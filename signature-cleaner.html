How to Capture and Clean Signatures for Online Forms

Online exam and recruitment portals across India (SSC CGL/CHSL, UPSC Civil Services, IBPS PO/Clerk, SBI, RRB NTPC, and state PSCs) require candidates to upload a clear signature on plain white paper.

1. Sign with the Right Pen

Use a smooth black or dark blue ballpoint pen or micro gel pen on crisp unruled A4 paper. Avoid pencils, highlighters, or fountain pens that bleed into paper fibers.

2. Photograph in Even Daylight

Place the paper on a flat table near a window. Avoid standing directly over the page to prevent your body or mobile phone from casting a harsh shadow.

3. Clean the Background

Upload the image to ApplyReady. Adjust the Paper Whiteness slider until all paper texture and shadows turn pure white (#FFFFFF), and ensure ink lines remain dark.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why do official exam portals reject photos of paper signatures?

Portals like SSC, UPSC, and IBPS use automated verification software. When candidates upload phone photos with dim room lighting, gray paper shadows, or yellow tints, the automated scanner cannot differentiate the ink from the background, resulting in application rejection.

Does this tool upload my signature to any server?

No. ApplyReady runs 100% locally on your computer or mobile device using HTML5 Canvas pixel manipulation. Your signature image is processed in your device's memory and is never uploaded, stored, or transmitted over the internet.

Should I choose JPEG or Transparent PNG for exam forms?

For Indian government recruitment portals (SSC, UPSC, RRB, IBPS), always choose JPEG with a crisp white background. Transparent PNGs are often converted to black background images by portal upload scripts. Choose Transparent PNG only for inserting your signature into digital PDFs or Word documents.

How do I remove phone camera shadows from my signature?

Increase the Paper Whiteness threshold slider to around 70–80%. The algorithm scans every pixel and converts gray and off-white background shadows to pure 255/255/255 white while preserving the dark ink lines.

Can I convert a faint blue ink signature to deep black?

Yes. Select 'Pure Black Ink' from the ink color option dropdown. The tool will convert all detected ink strokes to high-contrast black while smoothing edges.
