How to get a clean signature file, step by step

The cleaner does one job: it turns a phone photo of a signature into dark ink on plain white paper. It does not resize the file for a form; that is the second step, in the image resizer or the signature resizer. The tutorial on making and resizing a signature image walks through both steps.

  1. Sign on plain white paper with a dark pen. Black ink on unlined white paper gives the cleanest result, and it is what forms usually ask for. Use your normal running signature, not your name in capital letters. Sign at your normal size or a little larger: a tiny signature becomes unreadable once it is shrunk to a few hundred pixels, so sign inside a light pencil box and let the signature fill most of it.
  2. Photograph it flat, in daylight, from straight above. Keep your hand and phone from casting a shadow across the page.
  3. Clean it here. Add the photo and raise Paper whiteness only if grey patches remain.
  4. Resize it for the form. Use the resize button under the cleaned image to open it in the image resizer, then enter the pixel size and KB range your form gives, for example 300 × 100 px and 10 to 20 KB. The resize to 20 KB page covers the most common signature limit.

A real before and after

We ran the cleaner on the test signature in our sample images: a 1600 × 900 px phone-style photo of blue ink on paper with a darker corner, 251.5 KB as a JPEG. With the default settings (paper whiteness 70, ink darkness 80, specks removed, original ink colour) the cleaned JPEG was still 1600 × 900 px and 44.1 KB. The same result saved as PNG was 151.7 KB, and as a transparent PNG 161.2 KB. None of these fits a 10 to 20 KB signature slot yet. Resized to 300 × 100 px in the resizer, the uncleaned photo came to 8.0 KB and the cleaned version to 6.4 KB, both at the resizer's top quality setting: plain white paper takes fewer bytes than grey, textured paper.

Common mistakes

  • Uploading the cleaned file as it is. It keeps the full camera resolution. Always resize to the form's limit afterwards.
  • Pushing whiteness too far. Thin strokes from a ballpoint can vanish. If loops break up, lower Paper whiteness and raise Ink darkness instead.
  • Choosing transparent PNG for a form. Forms that ask for JPEG will not accept PNG, and a transparent background can show up as black when a site converts it. Use transparent PNG only to place your signature on your own documents.

When not to use this tool

  • When the form wants a scan of a signature inside a printed box or on a specific form page; upload that page as it is.
  • When you need a legally valid electronic signature on a document. A picture of a signature is not a digital signature certificate; see signature images versus digital signatures.
  • On anyone's signature but your own. Application forms require the signature to be the applicant's own.

Questions

Does this tool upload my signature to a server?

No. The cleaning runs in your browser on your device. The image is not sent to ApplyReady or anyone else, and the tool keeps working offline once the page has loaded.

Should I choose JPEG or PNG for an application form?

Choose JPEG unless the form names another format. Forms that ask for a signature image usually want JPEG/JPG. Keep PNG and transparent PNG for placing your signature on your own documents.

How do I remove a shadow from the signature photo?

Raise Paper whiteness a little at a time until the grey patch turns white, then check that thin strokes are still visible. If they start to break up, retake the photo in daylight without your hand or phone over the page; that works better than any setting.

Can I turn blue ink into black?

Yes. Choose black in the Ink colour menu. Check your form first: some forms ask for black ink on the paper, which a colour change after the fact does not replace.

Why is the cleaned file still too large for the form?

The cleaner keeps the full resolution of your photo. In our test, a 1600 × 900 px signature came out at 44.1 KB as JPEG. Use the resize button under the cleaned image to bring it to the pixel size and KB range your form asks for.

Is a cleaned signature image a digital signature?

No. It is a picture of your handwriting. A digital signature is created with a digital signature certificate and is tied to the content of a document; see the guide on signature images and digital signatures.
