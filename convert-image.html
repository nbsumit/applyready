Image converter

Convert photos and graphics between JPG, PNG and WebP. The pixel size stays the same unless you choose otherwise. Your images stay on this device.

      Which format to choose

      • JPEG (.jpg) for photos and for any application form that does not say otherwise. It is accepted almost everywhere and makes small files.
      • PNG (.png) for screenshots, logos and drawings with sharp edges, and whenever you need a transparent background. Photos saved as PNG are large.
      • WebP (.webp) is small and sharp, but many portals still refuse it. Use it for your own website, not for forms.

      Converting does not add detail: a JPEG turned into PNG looks the same and takes more space. The guide to image formats explains the differences in more depth.

      Transparent images

      JPEG has no transparency. When a PNG or WebP with a transparent background is saved as JPEG, the transparent parts are filled with the Background colour, white unless you choose another. Without that, many programs would show those areas as black. PNG and WebP keep transparency as it is.

      iPhone photos (HEIC)

      iPhones save photos as HEIC by default. Most browsers other than Safari cannot open HEIC, so this converter cannot read them reliably, and a HEIC file is refused with a message rather than converted badly. To get JPEG photos from an iPhone, open Settings, then Camera, then Formats, and choose Most Compatible; new photos are then saved as JPEG. For photos you already took, sharing them by email or choosing them in a website's file picker often gives a JPEG copy.

      Good to know

      • An animated GIF becomes a still image of its first frame.
      • Some browsers cannot save WebP. If yours cannot, the row says so; choose JPEG or PNG instead.
      • Every result is a new file, so location (GPS), camera and other hidden details are removed.
      • To make files smaller as well, set a maximum under More settings, or open the image compressor. To convert and resize a whole folder for a form, use the batch image processor.

      Signatures photographed on paper convert better after cleaning: run them through the signature cleaner first, then the signature resizer for the form's size.

      Questions

      How do I convert PNG to JPG?

      Choose JPEG under Save as, add the PNG files and press Convert. The pixel size stays the same. Transparent areas are filled with the background colour, white unless you choose another, because JPEG cannot store transparency.

      What happens to transparent areas?

      Saved as PNG or WebP, transparency is kept. Saved as JPEG, transparent areas become the background colour you choose under Format. White is the default, which suits most forms and documents.

      Can I convert HEIC photos from an iPhone?

      Not reliably. Most browsers other than Safari cannot open HEIC, so the converter refuses HEIC files with a message instead of producing a broken result. On the iPhone, choose Settings, Camera, Formats, Most Compatible to take new photos as JPEG.

      Does converting JPG to PNG improve quality?

      No. Detail lost when the JPEG was made cannot be brought back. The PNG looks the same and is usually several times larger. Convert to PNG only when a form or program asks for it.

      Can I convert many images at once?

      Yes. Add up to 500 images, choose the format once and press Convert. Each image gets its own row and result, and Download all as ZIP saves them together with the new file extensions.
