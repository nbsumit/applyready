Editorial policy

How the specifications, measurements and advice on ApplyReady are sourced, checked and corrected.

By Sumit Sengar | Published 24 October 2026 | Last updated 24 October 2026

What we publish

ApplyReady publishes three kinds of pages: free tools that run in the browser (image resizer, signature cleaner, passport photo sheet, image to PDF, PDF compressor and resume builder), exam pages that list the photo and signature rules of one exam, and guides that explain one task, such as getting a photo under a file-size limit or choosing between a one-page and a two-page resume. Every page is written and maintained by Sumit Sengar; the about page describes who he is, the project and its history, and the guides hub lists every guide with its last update.

How specifications are sourced

  • Official documents only. A pixel size, KB limit, dimension, date or notice number appears on this site only if it is stated in a document published by the body that runs the exam or issues the document: the exam notice, information bulletin, application instructions or the body's official website.
  • Each figure is cited. Exam tables give the notice name, its year and a link to the official PDF or page for every row. Guides end with a Sources section that lists the official documents they rely on.
  • No number when we cannot verify one. If an official document does not state a figure, or the document could not be opened, the page says "Check the latest official notification" and links the official site. We do not fill gaps with figures from coaching sites, forums or news articles.
  • The tools' presets are not official specifications. Where a notice gives a KB limit but no pixel size, the resizer still needs a pixel size to produce a file. The page says which size the preset uses and that the notice does not fix one.

How measurements are made

When a page says how large a file becomes at a given pixel size, or which quality setting the compressor chose, the figure was produced by running ApplyReady's own tools on the test images in /img/samples/. The test images are synthetic (no real person or real signature) and are generated by a script in the project repository, so anyone can repeat the measurement. Results depend on the browser's image encoder; the published measurements were made in Chromium 141 on Linux on 24 October 2026, and other browsers can produce files a few kilobytes larger or smaller at the same setting.

How often pages are checked

An exam page is checked again whenever that exam body publishes a new notice or bulletin, and every exam page is reviewed at least once every three months. Each exam page shows the notice it was last checked against and the date of that check. Guides show their publication date and the date of their last real content change; the "Last updated" date is not changed for layout or wording fixes.

How to report an error, and what happens next

Every exam page and guide has a "Report an error" link. You can also email hello@nbsumit.com with the subject "Report a wrong spec". Please include the page, what is wrong, and a link to the official notice that shows the correct figure. Do not send your documents or photos; we never need them.

We check the report against the official document. If the page is wrong, it is corrected, its "Last updated" or "Last checked" date changes, and the correction is added to the log below.

Use of AI tools

Parts of this site's code and text were drafted with the help of an AI assistant (Anthropic's Claude); the project's public Git history records which changes were made this way. AI output is never used as a source for a specification: every figure on an exam page is checked against the official document linked beside it, and measurements come from running the tools, not from a model.

Independence and funding

ApplyReady is not affiliated with, endorsed by or paid by the Staff Selection Commission, UPSC, IBPS, the National Testing Agency, the Railway Recruitment Boards, UIDAI, the Passport Seva programme or any employer. Their names appear only to describe the requirements candidates must meet.

ApplyReady is free and has no ads. There are no paid placements, sponsored reviews or affiliate links, so no one pays to influence what a page says. The privacy policy explains that the site sets no cookies.
