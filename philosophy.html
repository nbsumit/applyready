Our philosophy

ApplyReady is free, open source and private by design. It is meant to be a dependable public utility, not a business that earns money from people looking for work.

Why it exists

Someone looking for a job may be out of work. A student may have no money to spare. Someone applying for their first job may never have written a resume, and someone at a cyber cafe may have twenty minutes and a few rupees to get everything ready. None of them should have to pay for basic tools, create an account, or hand their identity documents to an unknown server. And nobody should lose an evening's work on a resume because a browser tab closed.

ApplyReady exists so that preparing a job application costs nothing and risks nothing.

Free, for everyone, forever

Every feature is free for every person, with no premium tier, no usage quotas, daily caps or paid limits, no watermarks and no "upgrade to download". The only limits are the technical ones each tool states, such as the largest file it accepts, which keep the browser from running out of memory. There are no subscriptions and no advertisements. The project will not add paid features later: a tool that people depend on while they are looking for work should not start charging them once they rely on it.

Private by design

Files are processed by your own browser, on your own device. ApplyReady has no server that receives your photos, documents or resume, so there is nothing to leak, sell or hand over. This is enforced, not just promised: the site's security policy stops the page from contacting any other website, and anyone can check the code. Resumes you save stay in your browser's storage on your device, and you can delete them at any time. The privacy policy lists exactly what is stored and where.

Open source

The complete source code is public on GitHub under the GNU Affero General Public License (AGPL-3.0). Anyone can read it to confirm that files are not uploaded, report a problem, suggest an improvement or contribute code. Open source also means ApplyReady cannot quietly become something else: if it ever stopped serving people well, anyone could take the code and keep the tools available. The AGPL also requires anyone who runs a modified copy as a website to publish their changes, so no one can turn ApplyReady into a closed or ad-filled product. The contribute page explains how to help.

Independent

ApplyReady is not owned by a recruiter, a job board, a resume company or an advertiser, and it is not affiliated with any employer, university or government body. It has no reason to steer you towards anything or to collect information about you.

Accessible and dependable

The tools are built to work on an inexpensive phone, on an old shared computer and with a keyboard or screen reader. A tool you have opened keeps working offline. Each tool checks its own result, shows you the exact size of the file it made, and says plainly when something cannot be done in a browser rather than producing a poor result.

How it is funded

ApplyReady costs little to run because it is a static website with no servers to pay for. Development time is the real cost. If you find ApplyReady useful and would like to support its continued development, you can contribute voluntarily. Your support helps us maintain and improve the platform. All features remain free, regardless of contributions: a contribution does not unlock anything, and not contributing never limits anything.

ApplyReady: free, open source, forever.
