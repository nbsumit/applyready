    How it works

    Every page is shown as a thumbnail. Drag a page by its handle to move it, or use the buttons under it: move earlier or later, rotate left or right, and delete. A deleted page stays in place, faded, until you save, so you can restore it. Insert pages from another PDF adds that file's pages at the end, ready to move. Nothing changes in your original file; Save PDF builds a new copy, opens it again and checks that it has the pages you kept.

    Rotation is added to any rotation a page already has, so a page that was stored turned still comes out the way you see it. Pages are copied, not redrawn, so text stays sharp. To join whole files, Merge PDF is quicker; to make several files out of one, use Split PDF; and once the order is right you can add page numbers.

    Limits

    Files up to 100 MB and 1,500 pages. Thumbnails are drawn only as you scroll to them, at low resolution, so long documents stay responsive. Bookmarks and form fields are not kept in the saved copy.

    Your files are processed in this browser and never leave your device. Closing the tab clears them.

    Common requests

    Rotate a sideways scan

    Use rotate left or rotate right under the sideways page until its thumbnail reads upright, then select Save PDF. Only that page turns; the others stay as they were.

    Remove a blank page or a back side

    Delete the page under its thumbnail. It stays in the grid, faded, so you can restore it until you save. Save PDF makes a copy without it.

    Put mark sheets in semester order

    Drag each page by its handle, or use the move buttons, until the order matches the form. The saved copy has the pages in the order of the grid, left to right and top to bottom; each thumbnail keeps its page number from your file, so you can see where every page came from.

    Questions

    How do I move a page?

    Drag it by the handle at its top left corner. With a keyboard, focus the handle and press the arrow keys to move it one place, or Home and End to move it to the start or the end. The move buttons under each page do the same.

    How do I turn a sideways page upright?

    Use rotate left or rotate right under the page, as many times as needed. The thumbnail turns so you can check it. Rotate all turns every page at once.

    I deleted the wrong page. Can I get it back?

    Yes. A deleted page stays in the grid, faded, with a Restore button, until you save. Undo all changes puts every page back as it was in your file.

    Can I add pages from another PDF?

    Yes. Select Insert pages from another PDF and choose the file. Its pages are added at the end, marked with the file name, and you can move them where they belong.

    Does it change my original file?

    No. Save PDF makes a new file that you download. Your original stays as it was.

    Is my PDF uploaded?

    No. Pages are drawn and rearranged by your browser on this device.
