The Ideal Target for Handwritten Declarations & Certificates

While signatures need under 20 KB and photos need under 50 KB, documents with multiple lines of text — such as the IBPS Handwritten Declaration (50–100 KB limit) and UPSC Civil Services uploads (20–300 KB limit) — require a higher data allowance to keep cursive handwriting legible.

Tips for Handwritten Declarations Under 100 KB

  • Write on Plain White Paper: Ruled/lined paper creates dark horizontal lines that waste precious bytes during JPEG encoding. Use plain white unruled paper.
  • Dark Black Pen: Use a dark black ballpoint pen for bold letter contrast.
  • Proper Cropping: Crop out tables, bedspreads, or desks from the photo frame so only the written text area is encoded.

Frequently Asked Questions: 100 KB File Compression

Common queries on 100 KB documents and declarations.

Why is 100 KB recommended for IBPS declarations?

IBPS specifies a file size range between 50 KB and 100 KB for handwritten declarations. Targeting close to 90–95 KB ensures high resolution (approx 800 × 400 pixels) so the verification officer can clearly read every word of your text.

Can I use this for UPSC square photos?

Yes. UPSC allows photos between 20 KB and 200 KB. Setting 100 KB produces a sharp square image well within official boundaries.
