How to Use ApplyReady

Comprehensive guide to resizing images under strict file-size limits and creating ATS-friendly resumes.

1. Resizing & Compressing Images

ApplyReady’s Image Resizer allows you to set exact output pixel dimensions (e.g. 350 × 450 px) and a strict maximum file size (e.g. 50 KB). Follow these steps:

  1. Choose what you are preparing: Pick the requirement from your form, such as ID / passport photo, Signature or Thumb impression, or pick Custom size to enter your own. Each option shows its pixel size and file limit. Exam and file-size pages open with their requirement already chosen; use Change to pick another. Check the requirements on the form you are applying to; a preset does not guarantee acceptance by a particular authority.
  2. Add your image: Drop a file into the workspace or select Choose image. You can also try the sample document. Supported files are JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF, BMP and AVIF, up to 50 MB and 64 megapixels. Animated files produce a still image.
  3. Position it in the frame: Drag the image inside the frame, and use the rotation (±90°) and zoom buttons. The frame always has the shape of the output, so nothing is stretched.
  4. Change size, file limit or format only if needed: Open Size, file limit and format to edit width, height, units, the minimum and maximum KB, the file format (JPEG for photos, PNG for graphics, WebP for the web) and the crop shape. Preset values stay editable; editing one switches to Custom size. Use original size keeps the whole image at its own dimensions. Output is limited to 20–8,000 pixels on each side and 32 megapixels.
  5. Optional background and name strip: Under Background and name strip, choose the fill for transparent areas when saving as JPEG, and add a name or date printed under the photo. The frame adjusts so the photo is not distorted.
  6. Resize and download: Select Resize and compress. The result shows the requirement next to your file (file size, dimensions and format), each one checked, with Download image first. If the limit cannot be met at your chosen dimensions, increase the limit, reduce the dimensions or try another format. ApplyReady does not silently shrink your output. Use Adjust again to change something, or Replace to start with another file.

2. Building an ATS-Friendly Resume

Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) and recruiters prefer clean, single-column documents formatted with standard font hierarchies and true text streams. Here is how to use the builder:

  1. Start with Sample or Blank: Click Try a sample to see a complete professional example, or start fresh with empty fields.
  2. Fill in Contact Details: Enter your name, target title, email, phone, location, and professional links. Separator bullets will automatically appear only between populated fields.
  3. Add Experience, Education & Projects: Use the Add buttons to create dynamic entries. You can reorder entries with the Move Up and Move Down buttons or delete unnecessary sections.
  4. Customize Skills and Headings: Enter your skills in up to four labelled rows. Rename any row label or section heading (for example Internships instead of Work Experience); headings you leave unchanged follow the selected template's wording.
  5. Choose a design: Open Style to choose one of 15 ATS-friendly templates (filter by General, Students & freshers, Tech, Senior or Specialist roles), preview it, set A4 or US Letter paper, change typography and reorder sections. Your entered content is preserved when switching templates.
  6. Save Local Drafts & Backup: In the Download step, under Keep your work, turn on Save a draft on this device on your own device. You can also use Save backup file to download a JSON copy, then restore it with Import backup.
  7. Compare with a job description: In the Review step, paste the job advert to see which of its key terms your resume already uses and which are missing. The comparison runs on your device and the advert is not saved. Add only terms that truly describe your experience.
  8. Review & download: Open Review to see what is missing, with a Fix button that goes to the field, then open Download to export PDF, editable Word or plain text. For characters outside the direct PDF engine's supported fonts, ApplyReady opens browser printing to preserve your text; choose Save as PDF. Review the downloaded file before submitting it.

Government Exam Photo & Signature Specifications

Specific guidelines and preloaded resizing tools for major competitive examinations:

SSC (CGL, CHSL, MTS, GD)

Photo: 20–50 KB (3.5×4.5 cm). Signature: 10–20 KB (4×2 cm).

 UPSC (Civil Services, NDA, CDS)

Square ratio 350×350 px, 20–300 KB limit, candidate name and date options.

 IBPS (PO, Clerk, SO & RRB)

Photo (20–50 KB), signature (10–20 KB), thumb impression & declaration.

 NEET-UG Medical Entrance

Passport (10–200 KB, 80% face), postcard photo & signature guidelines.

 Railway RRB (NTPC, ALP, Group D)

Official photo (30–70 KB) and signature (30–49 KB) parameters for Railway recruitments.
