A few writing tips

Use the STAR Bullet Formula: [Action Verb] + [Context / Challenge] + [Quantified Result / Metric].

Strong Action Verbs: Directed, Orchestrated, Engineered, Spearheaded, Accelerated, Formulated, Overhauled, Optimized, Standardized, Consolidated.

ATS Formatting Tip: Keep sections standard and linear. Avoid graphic skill ratings, tables, and photos which many recruitment scanners cannot extract.
