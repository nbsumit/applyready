CV vs Resume in India: Key Differences for Job Applications

Confused between a Curriculum Vitae, a Resume, and a Bio-data? Learn what Indian corporate recruiters actually expect when they ask for your "CV".

In India, job seekers and recruiters frequently use the terms "Resume", "CV", and "Bio-data" interchangeably. You might receive an email from a Bengaluru tech recruiter saying “Please share your updated CV for the Frontend Developer role.”

However, in international and formal corporate hiring practices, a CV and a Resume are two fundamentally different documents with vastly different structures, page counts, and purposes. Submitting the wrong format can cost you an interview opportunity.

Direct Comparison: Resume vs CV vs Bio-data

Feature Resume Curriculum Vitae (CV) Bio-data (Biographical Data)
Length Strictly 1 page (max 2 pages for senior roles) 3 to 10+ pages (comprehensive) 1 to 2 pages
Primary Target Private corporate jobs, IT, MNCs, startups, banking Academia, PhD scholars, scientific research (CSIR/DRDO), medical Government clerical posts, defense commissions, matrimonial
Focus Competencies, quantifiable business impact, tech stack Peer-reviewed publications, grants, conferences, teaching history Personal traits: DOB, marital status, gender, religion, family
ATS Scanning Heavily parsed by enterprise ATS systems (Workday, Taleo) Usually reviewed by academic peer committees Manual clerical verification

1. The Resume: The Gold Standard for Indian Corporate Hiring

A resume is a concise, highly targeted marketing document summarizing your education, skills, and work accomplishments.

  • The 1-Page Golden Rule: If you are a college fresher, graduate, or professional with under 5 years of experience, your resume must be exactly one page. Recruiters spend only 6 to 8 seconds scanning. Spreading a fresher's credentials across two pages dilutes impact and signals poor communication brevity.
  • Tailored to the Job Description: A resume is dynamic. You adjust your summary, skills, and project bullet points to mirror the keywords of the specific role you are targeting.
  • Measurable Impact: Every bullet point highlights quantifiable outcomes (e.g., “Reduced database query latency by 32%” or “Managed a team of 4 engineers”) rather than listing passive job duties.

2. The Curriculum Vitae (CV): For Academia and Research

Derived from the Latin phrase meaning "course of life", a true CV is a chronological and exhaustive record of your entire academic and scholarly career.

You should prepare a multi-page CV if you are:

  • Applying for Assistant Professor or Lecturer positions in Indian universities (UGC, IITs, NITs, central universities).
  • Submitting fellowship applications (e.g. PMRF, CSIR-NET, ICMR, DST INSPIRE).
  • Applying for international Master's or PhD programs (US, UK, Germany, Canada).
  • A medical doctor or clinical specialist detailing residencies, clinical rotations, and surgical procedures.

A CV contains sections that would never belong on a corporate resume: Published Papers (with DOI citations), Conference Presentations, Book Chapters, Research Grants Awarded, Teaching Experience, and Academic References.

3. What Is a "Bio-data"?

"Bio-data" (Biographical Data) is a legacy document format widely used in India during the 20th century. It places heavy emphasis on personal demographics: Date of Birth, Gender, Father's Name, Religion, Caste, Marital Status, and Permanent Address.

Never submit a Bio-data for modern corporate or tech jobs!

Multinational corporations and Indian tech startups enforce strict anti-bias hiring policies. Including personal details like marital status, religion, caste, or a physical photograph can cause automated screening systems to flag your submission to avoid discrimination liabilities.

When an Indian Recruiter Says "Send Your CV", What Do They Mean?

In 90% of instances in the Indian private sector, the recruiter means: Send your 1-page modern resume!

Because Indian English traditionally inherited British terminology where "CV" is the common colloquial term for a resume, recruiters routinely say "Send your CV" when they actually want a standard ATS-optimized resume.

If you send an 8-page academic CV detailing every semester project, seminar, and college event to a private software company or bank, the recruiter will not read it. Always default to a clean, single-column 1-page resume unless the job listing explicitly specifies an academic or research post.
