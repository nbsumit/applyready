Guides

Guides for Online Exam and Job Applications

Twenty-one guides, each answering one question about photos, signatures, documents or resumes, with official sources and measurements from our own tools.

How these guides are written

Each guide answers one question that comes up when you apply for an exam or a job online: why a portal refused a photo, how to get a signature under 20 KB without losing the ink, how to put six certificates into one readable PDF, or whether a resume should run to a second page. They are written for people filling in the form themselves, often on a phone, often close to a deadline.

Where a guide gives a rule, such as a file size, a pixel size or a background colour, it quotes the official notice or instruction page and links to it in a Sources section at the end. Where it gives a measurement, such as how large a photo becomes at a given size, the figure comes from running ApplyReady's own tools on our published test images, and the guide says so. Advice that is a matter of judgement, such as how to word a resume bullet, is presented as advice, with the reasoning.

The guides are grouped by task below. Each shows when it was last updated; a guide is updated when its content changes, not for layout fixes. If something is out of date or wrong, every guide has a link to report it, and corrections are listed in the editorial policy. For the exam-by-exam rules themselves, start with the SSC, UPSC, IBPS, NEET or RRB page.
