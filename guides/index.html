How these guides are written

Each guide answers one question that comes up when you apply for a job, an internship or a university place online: how to make a resume that applicant tracking systems can read, why a portal refused a photo, how to get a signature under 20 KB without losing the ink, how to put several certificates into one readable PDF, or whether a resume should run to a second page. They are written for job seekers, students, freshers, working professionals and the cyber cafe operators who help them, often on a phone and often close to a deadline.

Where a guide gives a measurement, such as how large a photo becomes at a given pixel size, the figure comes from running ApplyReady's own tools in a current browser, and the guide says so. Where it relies on outside material, such as an image format specification or official advice on online safety, it links to it in a Sources section at the end. Advice that is a matter of judgement, such as how to word a resume bullet, is presented as advice, with the reasoning. Every portal sets its own limits, so the guides use clearly illustrative numbers and ask you to check the form in front of you.

The guides are grouped by task below. Each shows when it was last updated. If something is out of date or wrong, every guide has a link to report it through the contact page. To start working on a file straight away, open the image resizer, the resume builder or image to PDF. All of them run in your browser, and your files are never uploaded.
