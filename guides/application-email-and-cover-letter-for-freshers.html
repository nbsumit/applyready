How to Write a Short Application Email or Cover Letter as a Fresher

Four short paragraphs, one clear request and one properly named attachment. Here is the structure, with an annotated example.

By Sumit Sengar | Published 11 October 2026 | Last updated 24 October 2026

Email or cover letter?

If a portal has a cover-letter field, paste a short version there. If you are applying by email, the email body is your cover letter. The National Careers Service describes a cover letter as "a short letter, usually 3 to 5 paragraphs long" and notes that "You can write it as an email if you're applying online" (How to write a cover letter). Do not leave the email empty with the letter only in an attachment: the reader may never open it.

The subject line

Use "Application for [role], [your name]", and add the reference number if the advert gives one. The cover-letter advice suggests mentioning "the job title, and reference number if there is one".

  • Application for Junior Accountant, Priya Sharma
  • Application: Software Trainee (Ref ST-24), Rahul Verma

Avoid one-word subjects such as "Job" or "Resume", and capital letters throughout.

Four short paragraphs

  1. Who you are and what you are applying for. Your course or current role, the position, and where you saw it: "Introduce yourself and explain how you found the advertised job."
  2. Why you fit. One or two real experiences linked to what the advert asks for: "Highlight the skills and experience you have that match what the employer is looking for." Results-based bullets make good raw material.
  3. Why this employer. One specific sentence showing you know what they do.
  4. The attachment and the ask. Your resume is attached; you would welcome the chance to discuss the role; thank them.

Address it to a person by name if the advert or company website gives one; the National Careers Service says "It's important to try to address your cover letter to someone by name", and to use "Dear Sir or Madam" only when you cannot find one. About 120 to 180 words fits on a phone screen without scrolling.

The attachment

  • Attach one resume as a PDF, unless the advert asks for Word. A PDF keeps its layout on every device; will an ATS read your resume explains the formats.
  • Name it Firstname_Lastname_Role.pdf, not "resume_final_v3".
  • Keep it small. A text-based resume PDF from the resume builder is usually tens of kilobytes; our sample was 12.2 KB.
  • Send the email to yourself first and open the attachment.

Before you press send

  • Check the recipient's name and the company name, and remove anything left from an earlier application.
  • Use a plain email address based on your name.
  • Be wary of requests for payment. The National Career Service warns: "Jobseekers should not pay fees to employers" (ncs.gov.in); staying safe with document uploads has more.

Not sure whether to send a CV or a resume? See CV, resume or bio-data.

Sources

  • National Careers Service (UK): How to write a cover letter, UK government careers service; quoted where its advice applies to applications in India
  • National Career Service (India), warning about fees
