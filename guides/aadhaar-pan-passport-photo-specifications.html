Aadhaar, PAN Card & Indian Passport Photo Specifications

A comprehensive breakdown of dimensions, DPI, file sizes, and background rules for India's primary government identification cards and travel documents.

Applying for a fresh passport, updating your PAN card, or fixing your Aadhaar details in India often stalls at the simplest step: photo and signature uploads. Because each issuing authority uses distinct validation software, a photo acceptable for PAN will be rejected by Passport Seva, while an Aadhaar update requires strict live or biometric compliance.

Below are the verified, official standards mandated by the Ministry of External Affairs (MEA), Income Tax Department (Protean/NSDL & UTIITSL), and the Unique Identification Authority of India (UIDAI).

1. Indian Passport Photo Specifications (Passport Seva Kendra)

The Ministry of External Affairs adheres to the International Civil Aviation Organization (ICAO) Doc 9303 standards. When submitting physical photographs at a Passport Seva Kendra (PSK) or uploading to the Passport Seva portal:

  • Dimensions: Standard Indian passport size is 35 mm × 45 mm (3.5 cm × 4.5 cm). If applying for international travel or miscellaneous services requiring square format, 51 mm × 51 mm (2 × 2 inches) is required.
  • Face Composition: The face must occupy 70% to 80% of the frame. The vertical distance from the top of the hair to the chin must measure between 25 mm and 35 mm.
  • Background: Solid, untextured plain white or light cream background. Blue, red, or patterned wallpapers are grounds for immediate rejection.
  • Facial Expression: Neutral expression with both eyes open, looking directly into the camera lens. The mouth must be completely closed—no smiling showing teeth, frowning, or squinting.
  • Eyes & Eyewear: Both ears and edges of the face must be clearly visible. Tinted or colored glasses, sunglasses, or thick black frames that obscure the eyes are strictly prohibited. Clear prescription glasses are permitted only if there is zero flash reflection on the lenses.
  • Head Coverings: Religious headwear (such as turbans or hijabs) is permitted, but the full face from the bottom of the chin to the top of the forehead and both side edges must remain unobstructed.
  • Print Quality: Must be printed on high-quality photographic paper (matte or semi-matte preferred over glossy to avoid fingerprint smudges during scanning).

2. PAN Card Photo & Signature Rules (Protean/NSDL & UTIITSL)

When applying for a new Permanent Account Number (PAN) via Form 49A / 49AA or applying for PAN correction, you must upload a digital photograph and a scanned signature:

PAN Photograph Rules

  • Format: JPEG (.jpg or .jpeg).
  • Resolution: 200 DPI or 300 DPI.
  • Dimensions: 3.5 cm × 2.5 cm. On the UTIITSL portal, the image is strictly validated against 213 × 213 pixels.
  • File Size: Maximum 50 KB. The ideal target is 20 KB to 45 KB. Files below 10 KB or above 50.1 KB fail portal validation.
  • Lighting & Head Position: Even lighting without harsh shadows on the cheeks or behind the neck. Frontal face facing forward.

PAN Scanned Signature Rules

  • Pen & Ink: Sign with a black ink pen on clean, unlined white copier paper. Blue ink signatures are frequently flagged by automated verifiers.
  • Dimensions: 4.5 cm width × 2.0 cm height (approximately 400 × 200 pixels at 200 DPI). UTIITSL accepts 213 × 400 pixels.
  • File Size: Less than 50 KB (ideally 10 KB to 20 KB).
  • Crucial Warning: Do not sign in ALL CAPITAL LETTERS unless that is your legal signature. Sign inside an imaginary box without touching the paper borders. Use our Signature Background Cleaner to remove grey shadows and yellowish paper tint.

3. Aadhaar Photo Guidelines (UIDAI Updates)

Unlike Passport and PAN applications, you cannot upload a digital selfie or scanned photograph online for your primary Aadhaar record. Under UIDAI regulations:

  • Live Capture Requirement: To update your Aadhaar photo, you must book an appointment and visit an official Aadhaar Seva Kendra (ASK) or an authorized bank/post office enrolment center.
  • Enrolment Operator Capture: The operator uses an ICAO-compliant digital camera mounted in front of a white screen backdrop. Biometric iris and fingerprint scanners are captured simultaneously.
  • Fee: The official UIDAI fee for a biometric update (including photo) is ₹100. No separate physical photo copy needs to be carried to the center.
  • Ordering Aadhaar PVC Card: Once updated, you can order a pocket-sized waterproof Aadhaar PVC Card from the official UIDAI website (`myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in`) for ₹50, which prints your updated photo with a holographic watermark.

Common Reasons Government Photos Get Rejected

Over 65% of rejected identity card applications stem from avoidable photograph flaws:

  1. Selfie Distortion: Smartphone wide-angle lenses held at arm's length distort facial proportions, enlarging the nose and narrowing the ears. Always have someone else take the picture from at least 1.5 to 2 meters away using a 2x zoom or standard lens.
  2. Uneven Backdrops: Standing in front of a domestic wall with switchboards, curtains, textures, or cast shadows ruins machine face-detection algorithms.
  3. Low DPI or Heavy Compression: Compressing an image blindly with social messaging apps downsamples the resolution to 72 DPI, causing jagged pixelation when the government card printer prints your PVC card.
  4. Cropping Too Tight: Cutting off the shoulders or the crown of the head causes facial landmark detectors to fail the ICAO compliance check.
