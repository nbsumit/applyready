Image compressor

Make photos and scans smaller in KB for an upload limit or an email. Set a maximum file size or a quality level, add one image or many, and download the results. Your images stay on this device.

    Add the images to make smaller

    JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF, BMP or AVIF, up to 50 MB each and 500 files at a time. You can paste images too. Processed on your device, never uploaded.

      How compression works here

      JPEG and WebP files have a quality setting: lower quality means fewer bytes and, eventually, visible blur and blocks. With a maximum set, the compressor tries each image at the quality you chose and, if the file is too large, searches for the highest quality that fits under the limit. The pixel size stays the same when it can. With Allow smaller dimensions on, quality is not taken below 50%: an image that still does not fit gets fewer pixels instead, because a smaller photo at decent quality looks far better than a full-size one full of blocks. Its row then says so. With the switch off, the pixel size never changes and quality can go as low as 5%. Leave the maximum empty to compress every image at one quality level instead.

      Every result is opened again and checked before it is listed as done, so the size shown is the real size of the file you download, in KB and in exact bytes. Portals count 1 KB as 1,024 bytes, and so does this page.

      PNG files

      PNG is lossless: it has no quality setting, so the only ways to make a PNG much smaller are fewer pixels or a different format. A photo saved as PNG usually shrinks to a fraction of its size as JPEG. Choose JPEG under Format for photos; keep PNG for screenshots and graphics with sharp edges or transparency. Converting is covered in more detail on the image converter page.

      Good to know

      • A photo that is already well compressed can come out larger. When the original already meets your settings and holds no hidden details, it is kept as it is and the row says so. When it holds hidden details, such as camera or location data, or is animated, you get the clean copy (for an animation, a still picture of its first frame), and the row says the original is smaller and why, so you can choose.
      • Each round of JPEG compression loses a little detail. Compress from the original photo, not from a file that was already shrunk.
      • The compressor writes new files, so location (GPS), camera and other hidden details in your photos are removed.
      • For a form that also fixes the pixel size, such as 350 × 450 px under 50 KB, use the passport photo resizer for one photo, or the batch image processor with the passport preset for many.

      To keep a photo sharp under a tight limit, read how to shrink a photo without blur. For a PDF instead of an image, use Compress PDF. Every click, with screenshots and what each note under a result means, is in the tutorial on compressing an image to a KB limit.

      When a form also fixes the pixel size, the image resizer does both in one step. Its pages for common limits open with the limit already set: resize to 20 KB, resize to 50 KB, and 10, 100, 200 KB or any other limit.

      Questions

      How do I compress a photo to under 100 KB?

      Type 100 in Maximum, add the photo and press Compress. The compressor finds the highest quality that fits under 100 KB and keeps the pixel size. The row shows the new size; if the quality had to drop a lot, it says so, so you can check the picture before you upload it.

      Will compressing change the pixel size?

      Only when it has to. The quality is lowered first, but not below 50% while Allow smaller dimensions is on: if the file still does not fit, the pixel size is reduced instead, and the row tells you. Turn the switch off if the pixel size must not change; the quality can then go much lower.

      Why is my PNG still large?

      PNG keeps every pixel exactly, so it has no quality setting to lower. Choose JPEG under Format to compress a photo, which usually makes it many times smaller. Keep PNG only for screenshots, logos and images that need transparency.

      Can I compress many images at once?

      Yes. Add up to 500 images, set the limit once and press Compress. Every image gets its own row with its result, and Download all as ZIP saves them together.

      Is my image uploaded to compress it?

      No. Compression happens in your browser on this device, and the page cannot send your files anywhere. Closing the tab discards everything.

      Your images are processed in this browser and never leave your device. Nothing is saved after you close the tab.
