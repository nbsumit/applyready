    Good to know

    • Every page keeps its size and orientation.
    • A photo or scan becomes one A4 page, fitted to the page.
    • Bookmarks and fillable form fields from the original files are not carried over.

    How it works

    Add the files, put them in order, and select Merge files. Each PDF is opened in your browser and its pages are copied, unchanged, into a new document: text stays selectable, and every page keeps its own size and orientation, so an A4 letter and a landscape certificate sit side by side correctly. A photo or scan becomes one A4 page, turned to suit the picture, and its metadata (camera details, the date taken and any GPS location) is left out of the PDF. Before the download appears, the new file is opened again and its page count is checked against the files you added.

    Portals that ask for "all documents in one PDF" usually also have a size limit. Merge first, then bring the whole file under the limit with Compress PDF. To take pages out, or to turn a sideways page, use Organise PDF pages on the merged file; to pull a few pages out of a long document before merging, use Split PDF. If you only have photos, Image to PDF gives you more control over page size and margins. Every step, including the password window, is shown with screenshots in the tutorial on merging PDF files.

    Limits

    Up to 50 files, 100 MB each and 500 MB in all, so the browser tab does not run out of memory. Bookmarks, links between documents and fillable form fields are not carried over; what the pages show is kept. A password-protected PDF can be added if you know its password, which is used only in this tab, and the merged file has no password.

    Your files are processed in this browser and never leave your device. Closing the tab clears them.

    Questions

    Does merging change how my pages look?

    No. Pages are copied into the new file as they are, with the same text, images, size and orientation. Nothing is redrawn or compressed, so the merged file is about as large as the files you added put together.

    Can I add photos of documents as well as PDFs?

    Yes. JPG, PNG and WebP images are accepted. Each image becomes one A4 page, portrait or landscape to suit the picture, with the image fitted to the page. Phone photos are turned upright first.

    How do I change the order?

    Drag a file by its handle, or use the up and down buttons on each row. With a keyboard, focus the handle and use the arrow keys, or Home and End. Files are joined from the top of the list to the bottom.

    One of my PDFs asks for a password. Can I still merge it?

    Yes, if you know the password. ApplyReady asks for it in a dialog and uses it only in this browser tab to open the file. The merged PDF does not need a password.

    Is there a limit on the number of files?

    Up to 50 files at a time, each up to 100 MB, and 500 MB in all. These limits keep your browser responsive, especially on phones. For more, merge in groups, then merge the results.

    Are my files uploaded?

    No. The PDFs are read and joined by your browser on this device. ApplyReady has no server that receives files, and the page cannot send data to any other address.
