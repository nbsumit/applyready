Common Reasons UPSC Uploads Get Rejected

  • Resolution Below 350 × 350 Pixels: The UPSC portal system blocks any image where either width or height is less than 350 pixels.
  • File Size Outside Allowed Limits: Uploads must be 20 KB – 200 KB for photographs and 20 KB – 100 KB for triple signatures. Compressed images under 20 KB or files over the limit will be rejected.
  • Single Signature Instead of Triple: UPSC mandates signing three times, one below the other, in black ballpoint ink on plain white paper within a single scanned image.
  • Signature in Blue or Gel Ink: UPSC explicitly advises signing with a black ballpoint pen on plain white paper for sharp contrast.

What the UPSC limits mean for your files

The sizes above are easy to read and hard to judge. Dividing each file-size range by the number of pixels shows how much detail each pixel can carry, and where the real risk is: a file that is too large, or one that comes out too small.

FileDimensionsSize rangeBits per pixel (low to high)What to watch
Scanned photo350 x 35020 to 200 KB1.3 to 13.4A very wide range. Aim for the upper half and keep the face sharp.
Triple signature, at 350 x 350350 x 35020 to 100 KB1.3 to 6.7Three signatures share one image: each gets roughly a third of the height.
Triple signature, at 500 x 500500 x 50020 to 100 KB0.7 to 3.3More pixels give each signature more room but less budget per pixel.

The photo has plenty of room. At 350 x 350 pixels the allowed range runs from about 1.3 bits per pixel at 20 KB to more than 13 at 200 KB. There is no benefit in landing near the bottom; a larger file gives a sharper face and is still inside the limit.

The triple signature is a layout problem first. The instruction on this page asks for three signatures, one below the other, in one image. At 350 x 350 pixels that leaves about 115 pixels of height for each signature. Sign small enough to fit three lines on the paper with clear gaps, crop to the block of three, and check that no signature touches the next one before you compress.

Always confirm the figures against the current official notification linked above before you upload. For the method behind these numbers, see pixels, centimetres and DPI and common upload errors and fixes.

Frequently Asked Questions: UPSC Document Uploads

Official requirements for CSE, NDA, and OTR registration.

What are the exact pixel dimensions required for UPSC?

For triple signatures, pixel dimensions must be between 350 × 350 px and 500 × 500 px. For photographs, verify dimensions against your examination notice (square 350 × 350 px is commonly recommended). ApplyReady defaults to 350 × 350 pixels to ensure swift compliance.

What is the file size limit for UPSC Civil Services?

The file size must be between 20 KB and 200 KB for photographs (and 20 KB to 100 KB for signatures) in JPEG/JPG format. Files outside these limits will be rejected by the portal validator.

Is my photograph private and secure?

Yes. All image cropping, dimension recalculation, and compression take place 100% locally in your web browser. No files are uploaded to any server.
