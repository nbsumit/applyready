Common Reasons UPSC Uploads Get Rejected

  • Resolution Below 350 × 350 Pixels: The UPSC portal system blocks any image where either width or height is less than 350 pixels.
  • Resolution Exceeding 1000 × 1000 Pixels: High-resolution mobile phone camera photos often exceed 1000 pixels and fail the upload test.
  • File Size Outside 20 KB – 300 KB: Overly compressed images under 20 KB and original camera images above 300 KB will not be uploaded.
  • Outdated Appearance: UPSC rules require the photo to show a contemporary likeness taken within 10 days before filling the application.
  • Signature in Blue or Gel Ink: UPSC explicitly advises signing with a black ballpoint pen on plain white paper for sharp contrast.

Frequently Asked Questions: UPSC Document Uploads

Official requirements for CSE, NDA, and OTR registration.

What are the exact pixel dimensions required for UPSC?

Both width and height must be between 350 and 1000 pixels in a square 1:1 aspect ratio (e.g. 350 × 350 px or 500 × 500 px). ApplyReady defaults to 350 × 350 pixels to ensure rapid processing while staying within the legal boundary.

What is the file size limit for UPSC Civil Services?

The file size must be between 20 KB and 300 KB in JPEG/JPG format. Files under 20 KB or above 300 KB will be rejected by the portal validator.

Is my photograph private and secure?

Yes. All image cropping, dimension recalculation, and compression take place 100% locally in your web browser. No files are uploaded to any server.
