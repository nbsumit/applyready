Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026. ApplyReady processes your files in your browser and never uploads them. It shows no advertisements, sets no cookies and uses no analytics. This page explains exactly what is kept on your device, and how to delete it.

In short

  • Your files never leave your device. Photos, signatures, PDFs and documents are opened and processed by your own browser. There is no ApplyReady server that receives them.
  • Saved resumes stay in your browser. They are kept in your browser's storage on this device so you can come back to them. You can delete them at any time.
  • No accounts, cookies, ads, analytics or tracking. The site cannot contact other websites: its security policy blocks it.
  • Browser storage is not a backup. It can be cleared by you, by the browser, or when a private window closes. Download a backup of anything important.

1. Files you open in a tool

When you choose or drop a file, the browser reads it from your device and the tool processes it in the page (with JavaScript, the Canvas API, Web Workers and the pdf.js and pdf-lib libraries, which are part of the site). The result is created in your browser and saved to your device when you download it. We do not transmit, receive, store or see your files. Files are kept in the tab's memory only while you work, and are gone when you remove them, reload the page or close the tab.

Applicant workspaces (the cyber cafe tool) keep every applicant's files in memory only. Nothing about a workspace is written to storage, and closing the tab erases it.

2. What is stored on your device

ApplyReady keeps a small amount of data in your browser on this device. None of it is sent to us or synchronised to other devices.

IndexedDB is a database built into every modern browser. Each website gets its own, which other websites cannot read. Data in it stays on the device where it was saved: it does not follow you to another phone or computer, and it is not backed up by ApplyReady.

3. Deleting your data

  • The Your data page lists everything ApplyReady has stored on this device and lets you export your resumes, delete them, clear recent tools, or delete everything at once.
  • In the resume builder, each resume can be deleted from My resumes.
  • Clearing your browser's site data for applyready.in (in the browser's settings) removes all of it.

4. What happens when browser data is cleared

Browser storage is convenient, not permanent. Your saved resumes are removed when you clear site data or browsing history, when you use a private or incognito window and close it, if the browser deletes data to free space (some browsers do this for sites you have not used for a while), or if you uninstall the browser. ApplyReady asks the browser to keep its data, but the browser decides. Download a backup of any resume you care about (My resumes or Your data, then "Export"), and keep it somewhere safe. A backup is a small file you can import again later, on any device.

5. Shared and public computers

On a cyber cafe, office or library computer, anyone who uses the same browser afterwards could open resumes saved in it. Turn off saving in the resume builder, use a private window, or delete your resumes before you leave (Your data, then "Delete everything"). Remember that downloaded files stay in the computer's downloads folder until you delete them.

6. Cookies and advertising

ApplyReady sets no cookies. ApplyReady shows no advertisements and loads no advertising or tracking scripts. Every page loads its scripts, styles, fonts and images from applyready.in only, and carries a Content Security Policy that stops the browser from loading code from, or sending data to, any other site.

7. Network requests and hosting

Your browser requests ApplyReady's own pages and files from its host, GitHub Pages, like any website. GitHub may log standard connection details (such as IP address, browser type and the time of the request) for security and reliability; see GitHub's privacy statement. These requests never contain your files or anything you type. Once a page has loaded, most tools work without a connection. The PDF tools also need their PDF library. Its main part is kept on this device once you have used a PDF tool online; the extra parts only some PDFs need (fonts, image decoders, character maps) are kept the first time a PDF needs them. Save every tool on the Your data page keeps all of it in advance except the character maps for Chinese, Japanese and Korean text, so such text needs a connection the first time it is read or drawn.

No analytics: ApplyReady does not use Google Analytics or any other analytics service, tracking pixels, session recording or error-reporting services. Even when a tool fails, nothing is reported to us.

8. Third parties

The site loads nothing from third parties: every page, style, script, font and image comes from applyready.in. Some pages link to other websites, and those open only if you follow the link: the source code on GitHub, the optional donation page hosted by Razorpay, and the official and reference pages that guides and help pages cite, such as government portals and browser documentation. If you contribute through Razorpay, Razorpay processes your payment details under its own terms and privacy policy; ApplyReady receives only what Razorpay shares with the page owner for a payment. A site you open through a link is governed by its own privacy policy.

9. Children

ApplyReady collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

10. Changes to this policy

If the policy changes, the date at the top changes and the changelog says what changed. The code is public, so anyone can check that the policy matches what the site does.

11. Contact

Questions about privacy: hello@nbsumit.com or the contact page. The source code is at github.com/nbsumit/applyready. The terms of use cover how the tools may be used, and the about page explains who maintains ApplyReady.
