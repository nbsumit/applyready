Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 12, 2026. Truthful, transparent disclosure of how ApplyReady handles data and advertising.

1. Core Privacy Principle: Zero Server Uploads

ApplyReady (https://applyready.in) is engineered to process your documents, images, signatures, and resumes entirely on your client device (laptop, phone, or tablet).

When you select an image file to resize or type your career history into the resume builder, that data remains strictly inside your web browser’s memory space. We do not transmit, upload, process, store, or view your uploaded images, signatures, or resume content on any external web server. All canvas processing, image cropping, and PDF/DOCX generation are performed 100% locally.

2. Local Storage & Draft Persistence

In the Resume Builder, an optional feature allows you to “Save draft on this device.”

  • How it works: If you explicitly enable this checkbox, your resume data is written to your browser’s local storage (localStorage) so that you can resume your session after closing the browser tab.
  • Where it lives: This data lives solely inside your local browser storage. It is never synchronized to the cloud or transferred across devices.
  • Shared Devices: If you are using a shared computer, cyber cafe kiosk, or public terminal, you should keep local saving disabled or click the “Delete Draft” button before closing your session.
  • JSON Backups: If you use the “Backup (JSON)” action, the resulting file is saved directly to your local file downloads.

3. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising Cookies

To maintain ApplyReady as a free resource for students and job applicants without charging fees, advertisements may be displayed provided by Google AdSense and third-party advertising partners.

  • Cookies & Ad Serving: Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies (including the DoubleClick cookie) to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites on the internet.
  • Personalized Advertising: Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to ApplyReady and/or other sites on the internet.
  • How to Opt Out: Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.
  • European Economic Area (EEA) & UK Users: In compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and UK GDPR, consent preferences for cookies and personalized ads can be managed via the AdSense privacy and messaging interface.

4. Analytics and Server Logs

Our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) may automatically log standard connection metadata, including your IP address, browser type, operating system, and request timestamps, to ensure infrastructure availability and security. These server logs do not and cannot contain the contents of your uploaded images or typed resume data.

No analytics tracking is used: We do not use Google Analytics, tracking pixels, or third-party session recording tools, and ApplyReady itself does not set tracking cookies. Cookies set by advertising partners are described in Section 3. Your activity on our tools remains unmonitored: the files you open and the text you type are never sent to us.

5. Offline Caching & Service Worker

ApplyReady uses a modern Service Worker to store site assets (HTML, stylesheets, scripts, and interface icons) in your browser’s local Cache Storage.

  • Purpose: Offline caching allows document preparation tools to remain fully functional without an active internet connection.
  • No Personal Data Cached: The Service Worker caches only static application code. It never stores, intercepts, or caches your uploaded images, signatures, resumes, or certificates.

6. External Links

Links you choose to follow to external sites, such as official recruitment portals (SSC, UPSC, IBPS, NTA), are governed by their respective privacy policies.

7. Contact & Inquiries

If you have questions regarding this privacy policy or our open-source codebase, you can reach out via our Contact page, by email at hello@nbsumit.com, or through our GitHub repository: github.com/nbsumit/applyready.
