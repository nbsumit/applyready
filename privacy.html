Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 24, 2026. Truthful, transparent disclosure of how ApplyReady handles data and advertising.

1. Core Privacy Principle: Zero Server Uploads

ApplyReady (https://applyready.in) is engineered to process your documents, images, signatures, and resumes entirely on your client device (laptop, phone, or tablet).

When you select an image file to resize or type your career history into the resume builder, that data remains strictly inside your web browser’s memory space. We do not transmit, upload, process, store, or view your uploaded images, signatures, or resume content on any external web server. All canvas processing, image cropping, and PDF/DOCX generation are performed 100% locally.

2. Data Stored on Your Device (localStorage)

ApplyReady stores two kinds of small settings in your browser's local storage (localStorage). They stay on your device and are never sent to us.

  • Theme preference: if you switch between the light and dark theme, your choice is saved under the key applyready_theme so the next page opens in the same theme.
  • Resume draft (optional): in the Resume Builder, the “Save a draft on this device” switch is off by default. If you turn it on, your resume text is saved under applyready_resume_draft (and the setting under applyready_draft_enabled) so you can continue after closing the tab. It is never synchronised to the cloud or to other devices.
  • Shared devices: on a shared computer, cyber café or public terminal, keep the draft switch off, or use “Delete draft” before you leave. Clearing your browser's site data also removes both settings.
  • Backup files: “Save backup file” downloads a JSON copy of your resume to your own downloads folder; we do not receive a copy.

3. Cookies

ApplyReady itself sets no cookies. The tools, the theme setting and the resume draft do not use cookies. Cookies on this site come only from Google AdSense, which pays for hosting by showing advertisements.

  • Advertising cookies: third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visits to ApplyReady and/or other sites on the internet. Google explains this in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and How Google uses cookies in advertising.
  • Consent in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland: visitors from these regions are shown a consent message from a Google-certified consent management platform (CMP) before personalised ads or advertising cookies are used, as required by Google's consent management requirements. You can change your choice later from the same message.
  • How to opt out: you can turn off personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings, or opt out of other vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at www.aboutads.info. Blocking or deleting cookies in your browser does not affect the tools.

4. Analytics and Server Logs

Our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) may automatically log standard connection metadata, including your IP address, browser type, operating system, and request timestamps, to ensure infrastructure availability and security. These server logs do not and cannot contain the contents of your uploaded images or typed resume data.

No analytics tracking is used: We do not use Google Analytics, tracking pixels, or third-party session recording tools, and ApplyReady itself does not set tracking cookies. Cookies set by Google AdSense are described in Section 3. Your activity on our tools remains unmonitored: the files you open and the text you type are never sent to us.

5. Offline Caching & Service Worker

ApplyReady uses a modern Service Worker to store site assets (HTML, stylesheets, scripts, and interface icons) in your browser’s local Cache Storage.

  • Purpose: Offline caching allows document preparation tools to remain fully functional without an active internet connection.
  • No Personal Data Cached: The Service Worker caches only static application code. It never stores, intercepts, or caches your uploaded images, signatures, resumes, or certificates.

6. External Links

Links you choose to follow to external sites, such as official recruitment portals (SSC, UPSC, IBPS, NTA), are governed by their respective privacy policies.

7. Contact & Inquiries

If you have questions regarding this privacy policy or our open-source codebase, you can reach out via our Contact page, by email at hello@nbsumit.com, or through our GitHub repository: github.com/nbsumit/applyready.
