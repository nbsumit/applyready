Contact

ApplyReady is run by one person, Sumit Sengar. Every message goes to hello@nbsumit.com.

What to contact us about

  • A wrong or outdated specification. Exam bodies change their photo and signature rules between notices. If an exam page gives a figure that differs from the current notice, email "Report a wrong spec". The template asks for the figure on our page, the figure in the notice and a link to the official notice, which is what we need to check and correct it. Corrections are listed in the corrections log.
  • A tool that did not work. First check the steps on the how to use page. If it still fails, Email a bug report with the tool, what you did, what happened, your device and browser, and the type and approximate size of the file.
  • A feature or a guide you would like. Email a feature request describing the form you are filling and the requirement you are stuck on.
  • Privacy. Email a privacy question about data stored on your device or the offline cache. The privacy policy answers the common ones.

Please do not send your documents

ApplyReady never asks for your photo, signature, certificates, identity documents or resume by email, and we will not open attachments of that kind. The tools work on your device, so we never need to see your files to help. If someone claiming to be ApplyReady asks for your documents or for a payment, it is not us.

Who runs the site and how it is funded is explained on the about page; the rules for using it are in the terms of use.

When to expect a reply

Messages are answered by email from hello@nbsumit.com, usually within 3 to 4 business days. A confirmed specification error is corrected on the page, the page's "Last checked" date is updated, and the change is added to the corrections log.

Write a message

Choose a subject and write your message; the button opens a new email in your mail app with the text filled in. Nothing is sent from this page.

Code and technical issues

The site's code is public. Technical bug reports and suggestions can also be opened as issues in the GitHub repository .
