Contact and support

Questions, problems and suggestions all go to hello@nbsumit.com. Technical issues can also be reported on GitHub.

Before you write

Most questions about a tool are answered on the how to use page and in the guides. If a portal refuses a file, the guide on fixing common upload errors matches each error message to its usual cause.

What to contact us about

  • A tool that did not work. Email a bug report with the tool, what you did, what happened, your device and browser, and the type and approximate size of the file.
  • A feature or a guide you would like. The feedback page explains what makes a useful report and offers GitHub as an alternative. Email a feature request describing what you are trying to prepare and where you got stuck.
  • Privacy. Email a privacy question. The privacy policy answers the common ones.

Please do not send your documents

ApplyReady never asks for your photo, signature, certificates, identity documents or resume by email, and we will not open attachments of that kind. The tools work on your device, so we never need to see your files to help. If someone claiming to be ApplyReady asks for your documents or for a payment, it is not us.

Who maintains ApplyReady and why it is free is explained on the about page; the rules for using it are in the terms of use.

When to expect a reply

ApplyReady is maintained by one person, so replies usually take 3 to 4 business days.

Write a message

Choose a subject and write your message; the button opens a new email in your mail app with the text filled in. Nothing is sent from this page.

Opens your email app with this message addressed to hello@nbsumit.com. Nothing is sent until you press send there. Up to 1,500 characters.

Code and technical issues

ApplyReady's code is public. Bug reports and suggestions can also be opened as issues in the GitHub repository . Please do not attach personal documents to an issue: issues are public. To help with code, testing or guides, see how to contribute.
