What the NEET (UG) 2026 bulletin asks for

NTA publishes the upload rules in the Information Bulletin NEET (UG)-2026, in Chapter 3 and again in the screenshots of the upload screen in its appendix. Page numbers below are the numbers printed at the foot of each bulletin page. NTA gives no pixel or centimetre size for any image, only a file size and format.

The signature limit changed in 2026. The 2025 bulletin gave the signature as 10 to 50 KB in one place and 4 to 30 KB in another. The 2026 bulletin says "Signature file should be in JPG/JPEG format between 10 kb to 100 kb", and the upload screen on p. 106 shows the same minimum and maximum. This page's preset follows the 2026 figure.

The fingerprint upload has two figures. The specification list (p. 16) and the upload screen (p. 106) say 10 to 200 KB, but a paragraph on p. 17 says "File size must be between 50 kb to 300 kb." A file between 50 and 200 KB satisfies both; if the portal shows a different limit when you apply, follow the portal.

The postcard photo is no longer uploaded. In 2025 candidates also uploaded a 4" × 6" postcard-size photo. The 2026 upload list and upload screen have no postcard item. A 4" × 6" colour print is still needed on exam day, pasted on the proforma downloaded with the admit card (p. 46).

The photograph rules in full

  • Recent: "taken after 01 January 2026" (p. 14). The 2025 rule was on or after 1 January 2025, so a photo from last year's application does not qualify.
  • Face and ears: "80% area of the image should be candidate's face" (p. 16). Crop tightly: a head-and-shoulders photo with a lot of background usually falls short of 80%.
  • Colour or black and white for the upload, against a white background (p. 16). The printed copies you carry must be colour with a white background (p. 10, p. 17).
  • Spectacles "are allowed only if being used regularly"; "Polaroid and Computer-generated photos are not acceptable"; the photographs "need not be attested" (p. 17).
  • The same photo everywhere: "Same Passport size is to be used for uploading with Online Application Form and for pasting on Attendance Sheet" (p. 17). The bulletin advises keeping 6 to 8 passport-size and 4 to 6 postcard-size colour prints.

The bulletin does not ask for your name and date on the photo. That instruction appeared only in the 2025 bulletin, as "preferably", and was removed in 2026.

Where the uploads come in the application

The bulletin's screenshots (pp. 99–107) show the order of the portal's sections. Photo capture and document upload come near the end, after the personal, exam-centre and qualification details:

  1. Registration and the form's detail sections.
  2. Capture Live Photograph (Step 14, p. 105).
  3. Upload Documents (Step 15, p. 106): photograph, signature, fingers and thumb impressions, then the certificates that apply.
  4. Final submit, then fee payment (p. 107). Once the fee is paid, the live photograph can no longer be changed (p. 9, item 16).

The bulletin warns: "Upload the correct photograph, signature, Left and Right Hands Fingers and Thumb impressions as the facility for correction will not be given" (p. 16). In 2026 NTA did open a correction window from 12 to 14 March in which the signature could be changed (public notice of 11 March 2026, Annexure-I); the photograph was not on that list.

Preparing the files with this tool

Because NTA sets no pixel size, the presets on this page are labelled "suggested": 400 × 500 px for the photo and 300 × 100 px for the signature. Any size works as long as the face is clear and the file is in range. At these sizes the files are comfortably inside the limits: our synthetic test portrait was 34.2 KB at 400 × 500 px and quality 0.95. Our test signature was 8.0 KB at 300 × 100 px, under the 10 KB minimum, so the preset padded it to 10.0 KB without changing the image.

The fingers and thumb impressions of both hands are one upload; the bulletin does not say how to lay them out or what paper to use. Photograph the prints in even light, choose Custom size, and set a minimum of 50 KB and a maximum of 200 KB, which satisfies both figures in the bulletin. The certificates are PDFs of 50 to 300 KB: image to PDF builds one from phone photos and compress PDF reduces a file that is too large. For photo framing, see taking a passport photo at home.

Frequently asked questions: NEET

What is the NEET 2026 photo size?

A passport-size photograph in JPG/JPEG of 10 KB to 200 KB, in colour or black and white against a white background, with 80% of the image showing the face including the ears and no mask. It must be taken after 1 January 2026 (Information Bulletin NEET (UG)-2026, pp. 14 and 16).

What is the NEET 2026 signature size?

JPG/JPEG of 10 KB to 100 KB (bulletin p. 16 and the upload screen on p. 106). Earlier bulletins gave smaller limits; use the figure in the bulletin for the year you apply.

Does NEET give a pixel size for the photo or signature?

No. The 2026 bulletin gives only the format and the file size. The 400 × 500 px and 300 × 100 px presets on this page are suggestions so the tool can produce a file.

Do I upload a postcard-size photo for NEET 2026?

No. The 2026 upload list has no postcard photo. You need a 4" × 6" colour print on exam day, pasted on the proforma downloaded with the admit card.

What file size should the fingerprint image be?

The bulletin's specification list and upload screen say 10 KB to 200 KB, while one paragraph says 50 KB to 300 KB. A file between 50 and 200 KB meets both; follow the limit the portal shows.

Can I correct my NEET photo after submitting?

The bulletin says correction will not be given for images. In 2026 a correction window (12–14 March) allowed the signature to be changed, but not the photograph.
