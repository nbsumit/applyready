NTA NEET-UG Image Specifications (from the official bulletin)

According to the latest NTA NEET-UG Information Bulletin, uploaded files must strictly satisfy the following criteria:

Postcard Photo Notice: Uploaded online during application (10–200 KB) and candidates must also bring a physical 4×6 inch printout to the examination centre to paste onto the printed Admit Card / Attendance Proforma. Always check the official NTA bulletin: exams.nta.ac.in/NEET.

Crucial Guidelines to Avoid NEET Form Rejection

  • Face Coverage Less Than 80%: Photographs where the candidate's face is small or indistinct are routinely flagged. Both ears must be clearly visible.
  • Colored or Textured Background: The background must be pure white. Blue, red, or natural outdoor backgrounds are strictly prohibited.
  • Name and Date on Photo: The NTA information bulletin specifies photographs should preferably indicate the candidate's name and date of photo at the bottom. Candidates are advised to include it or check the official bulletin to ensure compliance.
  • Signatures in Capital Letters: Signing in ALL-CAPS violates NTA instructions. Signatures must be in natural cursive handwriting.
  • Blurry Thumb Impressions: Use a quality blue stamp pad without excess liquid ink to keep fingerprint ridges sharp.

What the NEET limits mean for your files

The sizes above are easy to read and hard to judge. Dividing each file-size range by the number of pixels shows how much detail each pixel can carry, and where the real risk is: a file that is too large, or one that comes out too small.

FileDimensionsSize rangeBits per pixel (low to high)What to watch
Passport photo400 x 50010 to 200 KB0.4 to 8.2A wide range. Stay in the upper half for a sharper face.
Postcard photo1200 x 180010 to 200 KB0.0 to 0.8Large image under a hard cap: expect noticeable compression.
Fingers and thumb impression800 x 40010 to 200 KB0.3 to 5.1Landscape. Clear ink on white paper is what matters.

The postcard photo is the demanding one. A 4 x 6 inch image at 1200 x 1800 pixels has 2.16 million pixels. Even at the top of the 200 KB range that is under 0.8 bits per pixel, so compression will be visible. Start from a sharp, evenly lit photograph, crop to the postcard ratio before resizing, and examine the result at 100 percent zoom. If the portal accepts a smaller pixel size for this field, a smaller size will look cleaner.

Check the signature limit in the bulletin. The signature row on this page notes that the size limit has changed between cycles. Read the figure in the current bulletin before you resize, and use the minimum and maximum it states.

Always confirm the figures against the current official notification linked above before you upload. For the method behind these numbers, see pixels, centimetres and DPI and common upload errors and fixes.

Frequently Asked Questions: NEET Application Photos

NTA specifications for passport, postcard photo and signature.

What is the exact photo requirement for NEET-UG?

The passport size photo must be between 10 KB and 200 KB in JPEG format, with a white background, 80% face coverage showing ears, and candidate name along with date printed at the bottom.

What is the signature file size limit for NEET?

The signature file size should be verified in the current official information bulletin (typically 10 KB to 50 KB, previously 4 KB to 30 KB; check exams.nta.ac.in/NEET). It must be signed in running hand with a black pen on white unlined paper.

Are spectacles allowed in NEET passport photographs?

Spectacles are allowed only if being used regularly by the candidate. Tinted or dark glasses and caps are strictly prohibited.
