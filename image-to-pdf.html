Tips for Creating Clean PDF Submissions for Online Exams

Recruitment bodies including UPSC, SSC, state PSCs, and public sector banks frequently ask applicants to upload all marksheets or category proofs combined as a single PDF.

Correct Document Order

Ensure your pages are ordered logically (e.g. 10th marksheet, 12th marksheet, graduation degree, caste certificate). Use our Up and Down buttons to arrange pages before compiling.

Standard A4 Paper Dimensions

Almost all official document scanners in India expect standard A4 paper size. Selecting A4 ensures your certificates print with uniform borders without being cropped.

Maintain Legibility

Select 'Balanced' or 'High Quality' compression to ensure roll numbers, dates of birth, and university stamps remain razor sharp when inspected by verification officers.

Before you convert: preparing images for a clean PDF

A converted PDF is only as good as the pictures that go into it. A few minutes of preparation prevents most re-uploads.

  • Photograph flat and square. Place the document on a plain surface, in even light, with the phone parallel to the page. Shadows and angled shots make text harder to read and files larger.
  • Fill the frame. Crop away the table or floor around the paper. Every extra pixel of background adds size without adding anything a reviewer needs.
  • Use one orientation. Rotate pages that were taken sideways before converting, so that every page opens the right way up for the person checking it.
  • Name files so they sort. If you add several images at once, the order follows the order you chose. Check the numbered list and use the arrows to fix it before you convert.
  • Mind the portal's limit. If the finished PDF is above the portal's size limit, run it through the PDF compressor, or choose a lower quality in the settings above and convert again.

Choosing page size and margins

A4 is the standard in India and the safe choice for almost every application and verification process. Use "fit to image" only when a portal asks for the original proportions. Add a small margin when the document will be printed, so that edges are not clipped by the printer.

Privacy

Your pictures are processed in the browser and are not uploaded. This matters for identity documents, mark sheets and photographs, which are exactly the files people hesitate to put on a website.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I combine multiple certificates into a single PDF?

Upload all your certificate photos or scans at once. You can drag or reorder them using the up and down buttons to match the required document sequence, choose A4 page size, and click 'Convert to PDF'.

Are my confidential documents uploaded to any remote server?

Never. ApplyReady builds the PDF binary directly inside your browser using client-side JavaScript. Your marksheets, degree certificates, and identity documents never leave your computer or phone.

What page size should I select for government job applications?

A4 (210 × 297 mm) is the standard paper format for Indian government recruitment portals including UPSC, SSC, state PSCs, and universities.

Can I convert landscape images without them getting cropped?

Yes. Set Orientation to 'Auto (Match Image)'. The tool will automatically orient wide photos horizontally and tall documents vertically so nothing gets cut off.

What image formats are supported?

You can upload standard JPEG (.jpg, .jpeg), PNG (.png), and modern WebP (.webp) images.

How can I reduce the PDF size if it exceeds portal limits?

Choose 'Compact (Medium Compression)' from the Image Quality dropdown before generating the PDF, or use our PDF Compressor tool to target exact KB limits like 200 KB or 500 KB.
