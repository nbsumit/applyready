Where a 20 KB limit usually appears

Twenty kilobytes is mostly a signature limit, and sometimes a limit for a small photo or thumbnail. Forms differ in whether they also set a minimum and whether they give a pixel size at all. The examples below are illustrative of the kind of rule you will meet; follow the one printed on your form.

UploadTypical ruleWhat to enter in the tool
Signature with a range10 to 20 KB, JPEG, for example 140 × 60 pxWidth and height from the form, minimum 10 KB, maximum 20 KB
Signature with a printed sizeUnder 20 KB, about 6.0 × 2.0 cmUnit set to cm, the DPI the form states (or 300), maximum 20 KB
Small photoUnder 20 KB, for example 200 × 230 pxThe exact pixel size, maximum 20 KB

When a form sets a minimum as well as a maximum, a signature under the minimum is rejected just like one over the maximum. If the form gives only centimetres, pixels, cm, DPI and KB explains the conversion, and signature images for online forms covers signing, scanning and cleaning before you resize.

What fits in 20 KB: measured with this tool

20 KB is 20,480 bytes. We ran this page's compressor, with a 20 KB maximum and JPEG output, on the two synthetic test images in our sample set: a 1200 × 1600 px portrait and a 1600 × 900 px phone photo of a signature. The tool always tries quality 0.95 first and only lowers it when the file is too large. Measured in Chromium 141 on 24 October 2026.

Two things stand out. First, at the pixel sizes forms usually ask for (140 × 60, 300 × 100, 400 × 200) a signature fits under 20 KB without any loss of quality at all. Second, the same 20 KB spread over a large image forces the quality down to 0.35–0.40, where edges turn blocky. If a 20 KB file looks poor, the pixel size is almost always the reason, not the limit. Cleaning the signature first helps too: our cleaned test signature was 6.3 KB at 300 × 100 px instead of 8.0 KB, using the signature cleaner.

Troubleshooting a 20 KB upload

  • "File size should be at least 10 KB". Small signatures often fall below a minimum: our 140 × 60 px test signature was only 3.1 KB. Enter the minimum in Minimum (KB); the tool then pads the JPEG with harmless comment data until it reaches it, without changing the image.
  • The portal rejects a file the tool shows as 20.0 KB. Compare the exact byte count under the size with the limit. 20 KB is 20,480 bytes when a kilobyte is 1,024 bytes; if you are unsure how a portal counts, set the maximum to 19 KB to leave a margin.
  • You chose PNG. PNG does not shrink by lowering quality: our 300 × 100 px signature needed 37.8 KB as PNG, and the tool says so instead of producing a file. Use JPEG, which most forms ask for anyway.
  • The signature looks tiny or blurred after upload. The crop left too much empty paper. Crop close to the ink so the signature fills most of its frame.
  • The photo looks blocky. A 20 KB photo only works at small pixel sizes. If the form asks for a photo under 20 KB, use the exact pixel size it states, not your camera's.

Need a different limit? The 50 KB page covers photos, and 10, 100 and 200 KB limits share one page. For the method behind these numbers, read how to shrink a photo without blur, and if a portal still refuses the file, photo upload errors and fixes.

Frequently asked questions: 20 KB

Which forms ask for a file under 20 KB?

Mostly signature uploads on online application forms, often as a range such as 10 to 20 KB. Some forms also use 20 KB for a small photo. Check the limit printed next to the upload field.

Is 20 KB the same as 20,000 bytes?

Not necessarily. With 1 KB counted as 1,024 bytes, 20 KB is 20,480 bytes. The result table shows the exact byte count; if you are unsure how a portal counts, set a slightly lower maximum such as 19 KB.

Can a passport-size photo fit in 20 KB?

Yes, at small pixel sizes. Our test portrait was 17.5 KB at 350 × 450 px and 9.1 KB at 200 × 230 px. At 1000 × 1333 px it only fitted at a much lower quality, so use the pixel size your form states.

My signature is under the minimum size. What do I do?

Enter the form's minimum, for example 10 KB, in Minimum (KB). The tool pads the JPEG with comment data to reach it without changing the picture.

Why can't I get a PNG under 20 KB?

PNG is lossless, so lowering the quality does not shrink it. Our 300 × 100 px test signature needed 37.8 KB as PNG. Choose JPEG.

Is my image uploaded anywhere?

No. The resizing and compression run in your browser on your device.
