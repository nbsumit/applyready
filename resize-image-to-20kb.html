Which official forms use a 20 KB limit

Twenty kilobytes is mostly a signature limit. These are the uploads we have checked in the official documents; the limits differ in their minimum and in whether a pixel size is given at all.

UploadOfficial limitSize givenSource
SSC signature (CGL, CHSL, MTS, GD and other 2025–26 exams)10 to 20 KB, JPEG/JPG"about 6.0 cm (width) x 2.0 cm (height)"; no pixel sizeSSC CGL 2026 notice, 21 May 2026, para 9.6
IBPS signature (PO, Clerk, RRB, SO, 2026–27 cycle)10 to 20 KB140 × 60 pxCRP PO/MT-XVI notification, 1 July 2026, Annexure III
PAN photograph, online application with a digital signatureMaximum 20 KB, JPEG, colour, 200 DPI3.5 × 2.5 cmProtean guidelines for the DSC-based PAN application

Two of these set a minimum as well as a maximum: an SSC or IBPS signature under 10 KB is rejected just like one over 20 KB. SSC gives only centimetres, and some of its notices' annexures say 4.0 × 2.0 cm instead of 6.0 × 2.0 cm; the SSC page explains that conflict. Other exams use different limits; see UPSC and NEET.

What fits in 20 KB: measured with this tool

20 KB is 20,480 bytes. We ran this page's compressor, with a 20 KB maximum and JPEG output, on the two synthetic test images in our sample set: a 1200 × 1600 px portrait and a 1600 × 900 px phone photo of a signature. The tool always tries quality 0.95 first and only lowers it when the file is too large. Measured in Chromium 141 on 24 October 2026.

Two things stand out. First, at the pixel sizes forms actually ask for (140 × 60, 300 × 100, 400 × 200) a signature fits under 20 KB without any loss of quality at all. Second, the same 20 KB spread over a large image forces the quality down to 0.35–0.40, where edges turn blocky. If a 20 KB file looks poor, the pixel size is almost always the reason, not the limit. Cleaning the signature first helps too: our cleaned test signature was 6.3 KB at 300 × 100 px instead of 8.0 KB.

Troubleshooting a 20 KB upload

  • "File size should be at least 10 KB". Small signatures fall below SSC's and IBPS's minimum: our 140 × 60 px test signature was only 3.1 KB. Enter 10 in Minimum (KB); the tool then pads the JPEG with harmless comment data until it reaches the minimum, without changing the image.
  • The portal rejects a file the tool shows as 20.0 KB. Compare the exact byte count under the size with the limit. 20 KB is 20,480 bytes when a kilobyte is 1,024 bytes; if you are unsure how a portal counts, set the maximum to 19 KB to leave a margin.
  • You chose PNG. PNG does not shrink by lowering quality: our 300 × 100 px signature needed 37.8 KB as PNG, and the tool says so instead of producing a file. Use JPEG, which both SSC and the PAN guidelines ask for anyway.
  • The signature looks tiny or blurred after upload. The crop left too much empty paper. Crop close to the ink so it fills the frame; SSC advises a signature that fills at least 80% of its box (SSC advisory, 15 August 2024).
  • The photo looks blocky. A 20 KB photo only works at small pixel sizes. If the form asks for a photo under 20 KB, use the exact pixel size it states, not your camera's.

Need a different limit? The 50 KB page covers photos, and 10, 100 and 200 KB limits share one page. For the method behind these numbers, read how to shrink a photo without blur.

Frequently asked questions: 20 KB

Which forms ask for a file under 20 KB?

Mostly signatures: SSC signatures must be 10 to 20 KB (JPEG/JPG) and IBPS signatures 10 to 20 KB at 140 × 60 px. The online PAN application with a digital signature allows a photograph of up to 20 KB. The table on this page links each official document.

Is 20 KB the same as 20,000 bytes?

Not necessarily. With 1 KB counted as 1,024 bytes, 20 KB is 20,480 bytes. The result table shows the exact byte count; if you are unsure how a portal counts, set a slightly lower maximum such as 19 KB.

Can a passport-size photo fit in 20 KB?

Yes, at small pixel sizes. Our test portrait was 17.5 KB at 350 × 450 px and 9.1 KB at 200 × 230 px. At 1000 × 1333 px it only fitted at a much lower quality, so use the pixel size your form states.

My signature is under the minimum size. What do I do?

Enter the form's minimum, for example 10 KB, in Minimum (KB). The tool pads the JPEG with comment data to reach it without changing the picture.

Why can't I get a PNG under 20 KB?

PNG is lossless, so lowering the quality does not shrink it. Our 300 × 100 px test signature needed 37.8 KB as PNG. Choose JPEG.

Is my image uploaded anywhere?

No. The resizing and compression run in your browser on your device.
