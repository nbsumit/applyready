About ApplyReady

Free, lightning-fast document and image preparation tools built on a foundation of absolute client-side privacy.

Our Mission

Preparing job applications, official submissions, and exam portal uploads in India often requires tedious technical adjustments: strict pixel dimensions, stringent maximum file sizes (e.g. under 50KB or 20KB for SSC, UPSC, and IBPS), and ATS-friendly resume formatting.

Most conventional web converters upload your sensitive personal documents, passport photos, signatures, and career resumes to remote cloud servers. ApplyReady was built on a fundamentally different foundation: 100% client-side execution. All image processing, cropping, compression, and PDF generation occur directly inside your browser on your device using standard HTML5 Canvas and modern JavaScript.

Why Client-Side Processing Matters

When you use ApplyReady:

  • Zero Data Uploads: No photos, signatures, or resumes are transmitted over the network to our servers.
  • Instant Execution: Without network upload/download latency, processing happens in milliseconds.
  • Offline Friendly: Once loaded, the utilities function without active internet connectivity.
  • Transparent Privacy: Your data remains strictly on your device. We do not store, catalog, or analyze user documents.

About the Author & Why I Built ApplyReady

ApplyReady was created and is actively maintained by Sumit Sengar, an independent software engineer and technology builder based in India.

The motivation behind ApplyReady came from witnessing firsthand the daily struggles of students, freshers, and government exam candidates in India. Every year, millions of applicants face arbitrary image upload requirements:

  • A portal demands a photo between 20 KB and 50 KB, while another requires a signature between 10 KB and 20 KB.
  • Many applicants without access to desktop photo editing software end up visiting cyber cafes, paying ₹50 to ₹100 just to resize a single photo or signature.
  • Common free online tools often upload sensitive ID photographs to untrusted third-party servers, embed watermarks, or trick users with deceptive "Download" buttons.

ApplyReady was built to solve this once and for all: a clean, fast, respectful web utility where anyone can resize their photos, prepare signatures, and build ATS-friendly resumes in seconds — completely free, without sign-ups, and without any file leaving their phone or laptop.

The Technologies Behind ApplyReady

ApplyReady is built with lightweight, modern web standards — zero heavy frameworks, zero backend servers, and zero bundlers:

  • HTML5 Canvas API: High-performance pixel manipulation, aspect-ratio scaling, and distortion-free rendering.
  • Cropper.js: Touch-friendly, accessible image cropping with 90-degree rotation and fine-grained framing.
  • Native Vector PDF Generation: Direct browser-side generation of searchable, selectable text PDFs conforming to ISO 32000 / PDF 1.4 standards with zero rasterization.
  • Pure Client-Side OOXML Engine: Generates valid Microsoft Word (.docx) documents directly in memory.
  • CSS Custom Properties: Smooth light and dark themes adhering to system preferences and WCAG AA accessibility contrast ratios.

How We Write and Check Our Guides

The guides describe how the ApplyReady tools actually behave, and each one is checked against the live tools with real photographs, signatures, scanned documents and resumes before it is published. We explain the reasoning behind each recommendation (for example, why a signature compresses far more easily than a photograph) instead of repeating generic advice.

  • Exam specifications: The photo, signature and file-size figures on each exam page are taken from that exam body's own notification and application instructions. Every exam page shows the month it was last checked.
  • Rules change every cycle: Portals update their requirements between notifications. Treat our figures as a starting point and always confirm them against the notification for the exam you are applying to.
  • Corrections: If a figure is out of date, use the contact page and choose "Suggest Exam Specification Update". We review every report and update the page when the official notice has changed.
