About ApplyReady

Free tools and guides for preparing the photo, signature, PDF and resume that Indian exam and job portals ask for. The files are processed in your browser and are not uploaded to us.

What ApplyReady is for

Online application forms in India rarely accept a photo straight from a phone. Each exam body publishes its own rules: a pixel size, a minimum and maximum file size in KB, a file type, sometimes a signature written three times or a handwritten declaration. The rules differ between exams (compare the SSC, UPSC, IBPS, RRB and NEET pages) and change between notices. ApplyReady turns those rules into settings and produces a file that meets them, then shows the exact pixel size and byte count so you can check it before uploading.

What the tools do, and what they do not

  • Image resizer: crops a photo or signature to an exact pixel size and keeps the file between a minimum and maximum KB limit, in JPEG, PNG or WebP. It can add a name and date strip under a photo.
  • Signature cleaner: turns a phone photo of a signature into dark ink on white paper.
  • Passport photo print: lays out copies of one photo on a 4 × 6 inch or A4 sheet for printing.
  • Image to PDF and Compress PDF: combine scans into one PDF and bring a PDF under a portal's size limit.
  • Resume builder: single-column resumes exported as PDF, Word and plain text.

The tools do not submit anything to an exam portal, and they cannot make a photo acceptable if the photo itself breaks a rule, such as a cap, dark glasses or a busy background. They do not read iPhone HEIC files, do not edit the text inside a PDF, and the resume builder does not write your resume for you or score it. A file that meets the published limits can still be rejected by a portal for reasons the notice does not state, so keep the original and check the notice for your exam.

Who makes it

ApplyReady is built and maintained by Sumit Sengar, an independent software engineer based in India. He started it after seeing candidates without photo-editing software pay a cyber café to resize a photo, or use free websites that upload their identity photos, add watermarks or hide the real download button among advertisements.

How the pages are checked

Exam figures are taken only from the exam body's own notice or bulletin, and each table row links to it. File-size examples come from running ApplyReady's own tools on the test images in /img/samples/. The editorial policy explains the process, how often pages are re-checked, how AI tools are used, and lists every correction. If a figure is wrong, please report it with a link to the official notice.

Privacy

Images, signatures, PDFs and resume text are processed by JavaScript in your browser. They are not sent to a server run by ApplyReady, and the tools keep working offline once a page has loaded. The resume builder saves a draft on your device only if you turn that option on. The privacy policy covers the advertising cookies and the offline cache.

Independence and funding

ApplyReady is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to the Staff Selection Commission, UPSC, IBPS, the National Testing Agency, the Railway Recruitment Boards or any other examination body, employer or government department. Their names appear only to describe the requirements candidates must meet.

The site is free and has no paid version. Hosting and the domain are paid for by advertising served by Google AdSense; advertisers do not influence what the pages say, and ads are not placed next to the tools' buttons.

How it is built

The site is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript hosted on GitHub Pages, with no server-side code. Images are processed with the browser's Canvas API, cropping uses the Cropper.js library, PDFs and Word files are written directly in the browser, and a service worker keeps the tools available offline.
