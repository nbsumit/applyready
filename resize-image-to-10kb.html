Tips for Sharp Results Under 10 KB

  • Tight Crop: Do not upload full A4 scans. Crop tightly around the signature or face so bytes aren't wasted encoding blank borders.
  • Avoid Unnecessary Megapixels: A signature viewed on an application PDF only needs about 140 × 60 or 300 × 100 pixels. Setting smaller dimensions ensures crisp ink lines without compression noise.
  • White Background Fill: If your signature photo has yellowish paper lighting, setting white background fill produces higher JPEG compression ratios.

How many pixels can 10 KB afford?

A 10 KB file is 10,240 bytes, or 81,920 bits. Divide that by the number of pixels and you get the bits each pixel can use. That single number tells you whether a target is easy, workable or a struggle before you touch a slider.

Rule of thumb for photographs: 2.5 or more bits per pixel is comfortable, 1.5 to 2.5 is workable, below 1.5 looks soft or blocky.

Signatures are easy at 10 KB. A 140 x 60 signature has only 8,400 pixels and most of them are white paper, so the budget per pixel is generous. The usual problem is not the maximum but a form that also sets a minimum: a very clean signature can come out smaller than the minimum size. If your form has a lower limit, enter it as well, and the resizer pads the file with harmless metadata so the image itself stays unchanged.

Portraits are the hard case. At 200 x 230 pixels a face gets under 2 bits per pixel, which looks soft. Dropping to about 150 x 180 raises the budget per pixel by roughly 70 percent and gives a cleaner result, so if the form allows a range of sizes, choose the smaller one. If the form fixes the size, use a tight crop and good light in the source photo, because noise and shadow eat bytes that detail needs.

  • Check the smallest line of the signature at 100 percent zoom: a continuous thin stroke is the quality test.
  • Do not upload a coloured scan: a plain white background and dark ink compress best.
  • Re-export from the original, not from a previously shrunk copy.

Frequently Asked Questions: 10 KB Image Compression

Everything you need to know about preparing sub-10 KB files.

Will my signature remain legible at under 10 KB?

Yes. By cropping closely around the pen strokes and choosing moderate pixel dimensions (such as 300 × 100 pixels), our canvas engine preserves sharp stroke contrast while keeping file size strictly under 10,240 bytes.

Can I compress PNG images to 10 KB?

PNG is a lossless format and usually cannot reach 10 KB for complex photos. We recommend JPEG (.jpg) for photographs and scanned signatures to reliably achieve under 10 KB file sizes.
