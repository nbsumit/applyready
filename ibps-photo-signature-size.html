What the IBPS notifications ask for

IBPS prints its upload rules in an annexure titled "Guidelines for Scanning and Upload of Documents". The figures are identical in all four 2026 notifications we read (PO/MT-XVI, Clerk CSA-XVI, RRBs-XV and SO SPL-XVI) and in the three from 2025. Quotes and page numbers below are from the CRP PO/MT-XVI notification (1 July 2026), Annexure III.

All four image files must be JPG or JPEG: "The image file should be JPG or JPEG format" (PDF p. 42). In the Clerk CSA-XVI notification the same rules are in Annexure IV rather than Annexure III.

This page offers a preset for each of the four images. Every preset uses the pixel size and KB range in the table, so these are the one exam page on ApplyReady where every preset figure is an official one.

The handwritten declaration

The declaration has the most rules of the four images: its wording, its language and the handwriting are all specified. The PO/MT-XVI, CSA-XVI and SPL-XVI notifications give this text:

"I, _______ (Name of the candidate), hereby declare that all the information submitted by me in the application form is correct, true and valid. I will present the supporting documents as and when required."

The RRBs-XV notification (1 September 2026) differs by one word: "I will submit the supporting documents". Copy the text from the notification for the exam you are applying for.

"The hand written declaration has to be in the candidate's hand writing and in English only. The text should NOT BE IN CAPITAL LETTERS. If it is written by anybody else and uploaded or in any other language, the application will be considered as invalid" (PDF p. 41). A candidate who cannot write may have the text typed and put a left thumb impression below it.

At 800 × 400 px the declaration must be at least 50 KB, which is large for black text on white paper. Our synthetic signature photo, resized to 800 × 400 px, came out at 55.6 KB at quality 0.95, just inside the range; a very clean scan can fall under 50 KB, and the preset then pads the JPEG to the minimum without changing the picture.

How the IBPS upload step behaves

  • It will not let you continue. "Until photographs and signature are as per the specifications provided, the system will not allow the candidate to move forward to the next stage of application."
  • Each file has its own link, and "if the file size and format are not as prescribed, an error message will be displayed." The preview after upload is there to check quality: "In case of unclear / smudged, the same may be re-uploaded."
  • Live photo. "Candidates will also be required to capture and upload their live photograph either by using webcam or mobile phone." Capture Photo uses the webcam; Click here to Scan shows a QR code for a phone.
  • The photo is checked again later. "At different stages of the selection process: Photograph and Biometric data (Right thumb impression or otherwise) may be captured/ verified ... Candidate must NOT change his/her appearance from the photo uploaded/ captured in the application."
  • The signature is compared on exam day. If the signature on the attendance sheet or call letter "does not match the signature uploaded, the applicant will be disqualified."

IBPS's own scanning advice is to set the scanner to at least 200 DPI and "True Colour", crop in the scanner to the edge of the item, then crop again to the final size. If you photograph the documents with a phone instead, see phone camera tips for document scans.

The minimums are the usual problem

IBPS sizes are small, so files rarely exceed the maximum. They fall under the minimum instead. We ran the presets on our synthetic test images in Chromium on 24 October 2026:

PresetSize at quality 0.95IBPS rangeWhat the tool did
Photo, 200 × 230 px9.1 KB20–50 KBPadded to 20.0 KB

Padding adds JPEG comment data to reach the minimum; the pixels are not changed. Do not reach the minimum by making the image larger than the stated pixels: IBPS asks for the image to be cropped "to the final size (as specified above)". For other limits see 20 KB and 50 KB, and combine the 10th certificate into one A4 PDF with image to PDF. The document checklist helps prepare all five uploads before you open the form.

Frequently asked questions: IBPS

What is the IBPS photo size?

200 × 230 pixels (preferred), 20 KB to 50 KB, JPG or JPEG, described as 4.5 cm × 3.5 cm. It must be a recent colour passport-style photo on a light, preferably white, background (CRP PO/MT-XVI, Annexure III).

What is the IBPS signature size?

140 × 60 pixels (preferred), 10 KB to 20 KB, signed with a black ink pen on white paper, and not in capital letters.

What are the thumb impression and declaration sizes?

Left thumb impression: 240 × 240 px in 200 DPI, 20 KB to 50 KB. Handwritten declaration: 800 × 400 px in 200 DPI, 50 KB to 100 KB. Both are JPG or JPEG.

Can I type the IBPS declaration?

No, unless you cannot write. It must be in your own handwriting, in English, not in capital letters; otherwise "the application will be considered as invalid". A candidate who cannot write may type it and put a left thumb impression below it.

My IBPS photo is smaller than 20 KB. What do I do?

Keep the 200 × 230 px size and raise the file to the minimum. The IBPS photo preset sets a 20 KB minimum and pads the JPEG with comment data, so the picture itself does not change.

Do I need to upload a 10th certificate?

Yes. All 2026 notifications require the SSC/SSLC/10th certificate as a PDF on A4 pages, no larger than 500 KB.
