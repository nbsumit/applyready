IBPS Banking Exam Specifications (from the official notification)

According to official IBPS Common Recruitment Process (CRP) notifications, uploaded files must meet the following exact specifications:

Document File Size Limit Dimensions Format Key Guidelines
Passport Photo 20 KB – 50 KB 200 × 230 px (4.5 × 3.5 cm) JPG / JPEG Light/white background, frontal face, eyes open, neutral look
Signature 10 KB – 20 KB 140 × 60 px (~4.0 × 2.0 cm) JPG / JPEG Black ink, white paper, running hand (CAPITAL letters rejected)
Left Thumb Impression 20 KB – 50 KB 240 × 240 px (3 × 3 cm) JPG / JPEG Blue or black ink, unsmudged ridges on white paper
Handwritten Declaration 50 KB – 100 KB 800 × 400 px (10 × 5 cm) JPG / JPEG Written in candidate's own English handwriting in black ink (no caps)

Common Mistakes Leading to IBPS Rejection

  • Signature in Capital Letters: IBPS application rules strictly reject signatures written in block letters. Sign naturally in running hand.
  • Wrong Ink Color: Signatures and handwritten declarations must be executed with black ink. Thumb impressions may use blue or black ink.
  • Exceeding 50 KB Limit for Photos: The online IBPS portal automatically blocks files above 50 KB during form submission.
  • Smudged Thumb Impression: Press gently on ink pad to produce clear ridge patterns without thick blotches of ink.
  • Declaration in Another Person's Writing: The declaration must be in the applicant's own handwriting and in English. Typed or third-party declarations result in disqualification.

What the IBPS limits mean for your files

The sizes above are easy to read and hard to judge. Dividing each file-size range by the number of pixels shows how much detail each pixel can carry, and where the real risk is: a file that is too large, or one that comes out too small.

FileDimensionsSize rangeBits per pixel (low to high)What to watch
Passport photo200 x 23020 to 50 KB3.6 to 8.9Generous. A sharp photo fits easily.
Signature140 x 6010 to 20 KB9.8 to 19.5Tiny image. The risk is a file below 10 KB, not above 20 KB.
Left thumb impression240 x 24020 to 50 KB2.8 to 7.1Clear ridges matter more than the size; avoid smudged ink.
Handwritten declaration800 x 40050 to 100 KB1.3 to 2.6Most pixels of the four. Keep the paper white and the ink dark.

The signature is only 8,400 pixels. Against a 10 to 20 KB range that is more than nine bits per pixel even at the lower limit, far more than a black-on-white signature naturally uses. Left alone, a file this small usually comes out below 10 KB and is rejected for being too small. The IBPS signature preset sets the 10 KB minimum, and the tool pads the file so the image is unchanged and the size is valid.

The declaration is the largest and the tightest. At 800 x 400 pixels it has 320,000 pixels, so a 50 to 100 KB budget is about 1.3 to 2.6 bits per pixel. Handwriting on a white page compresses well, but only if the page is flat, evenly lit and written in dark ink. Re-photograph rather than push the compression if the writing looks faint.

Always confirm the figures against the current official notification linked above before you upload. For the method behind these numbers, see pixels, centimetres and DPI and common upload errors and fixes.

Frequently Asked Questions: IBPS Upload Guidelines

Clear specifications for PO, Clerk, Specialist Officer and RRB forms.

What is the exact photo size for IBPS banking exams?

The photo must be in JPEG format, 20 KB to 50 KB, with dimensions of 200 × 230 pixels (4.5 cm × 3.5 cm) against a light or plain white background.

Can I sign with blue ink for IBPS applications?

No. Official IBPS guidelines stipulate signing with black ink on white unruled paper in running handwriting. Left thumb impressions may be in blue or black ink.

What is the file size for IBPS handwritten declaration?

The handwritten declaration must be between 50 KB and 100 KB in JPEG format, with dimensions around 800 × 400 pixels (10 cm × 5 cm).
