Railway Recruitment Board Image Specifications (from the official notification)

According to recent CEN notifications on the official RRB recruitment portal, uploaded files must meet the following exact standards:

Document File Size Limit Dimensions Format Key Guidelines
Passport Photo 30 KB – 70 KB 240 × 320 px (portrait, 35 × 45 mm) JPG / JPEG Plain white background, full frontal view, no cap or dark spectacles
Signature 30 KB – 49 KB 300 × 120 px (minimum 140 × 60 px) JPG / JPEG Black or blue ink on plain white paper, cursive/running handwriting (no all-caps)

Key Guidelines to Avoid RRB Application Cancellation

  • File Size Outside 30 KB to 70 KB: Note that RRB has a higher minimum threshold (30 KB) than SSC or IBPS. Files below 30 KB are rejected.
  • Unacceptable Background: RRB insists on a plain white background. Colored walls, scenic photos, or shadows behind the face cause rejection.
  • Signatures in Capital Letters: Signing in ALL-CAPS is not accepted. Your signature must be in your regular cursive writing.
  • Accessories: Caps, hats, dark sunglasses, or heavy tinted glasses are strictly forbidden.
  • Unclear Signatures: Crop closely around the ink strokes to maintain crisp lines without excessive blank paper margins.

What the RRB limits mean for your files

The sizes above are easy to read and hard to judge. Dividing each file-size range by the number of pixels shows how much detail each pixel can carry, and where the real risk is: a file that is too large, or one that comes out too small.

FileDimensionsSize rangeBits per pixel (low to high)What to watch
Passport photo240 x 32030 to 70 KB3.2 to 7.5Generous per pixel. The risk is a file below 30 KB.
Signature300 x 12030 to 49 KB6.8 to 11.2Very generous per pixel. A clean signature rarely reaches 30 KB naturally.

Both RRB files have high minimums. A 240 x 320 photo has 76,800 pixels, so 30 KB already means more than three bits per pixel, and a 300 x 120 signature at 30 KB means nearly seven. A normal JPEG of either would often come out smaller than the minimum, which is why files are rejected for being too small rather than too large.

What to do. Use the RRB presets above, which carry the minimum and maximum, and let the tool bring the file into range. After resizing, check the exact byte size shown in the result, and confirm it is between the minimum and the maximum in the current notice.

Always confirm the figures against the current official notification linked above before you upload. For the method behind these numbers, see pixels, centimetres and DPI and common upload errors and fixes.

Frequently Asked Questions: RRB Document Resizing

Official requirements for Railway ALP, NTPC and Technician exams.

What is the minimum file size for RRB photographs?

For RRB, photos must be 30–70 KB (portrait, 240 × 320 px). Per official CEN notices, signatures must be strictly between 30 KB and 49 KB.

What are the dimensions for RRB photo and signature?

The photograph dimensions are 240 × 320 pixels (portrait, 35 mm × 45 mm). The signature requires minimum 140 × 60 pixels (up to 300 × 120 pixels).

Is my data stored on any server when using ApplyReady?

No. ApplyReady runs entirely in your local web browser. Your photos and signatures never leave your device.
