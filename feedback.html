Feedback

Found a problem or have an idea? Tell us by email or on GitHub. This page collects nothing: you decide what to send and where.

Report a problem

The most useful report says which tool you used, what you did, what you expected and what happened, plus your device and browser (for example "Android phone, Chrome"). Please describe the file instead of attaching it: its type and roughly how large it is. Never send identity documents, certificates or a real resume.

  • By email: write a bug report (opens your email app with the questions filled in).
  • On GitHub: open a bug report. GitHub issues are public, so do not include personal details.

Suggest a feature or a guide

Tell us what you were trying to prepare and where you got stuck; that helps more than a feature name. Suggestions are weighed against ApplyReady's principles: it must be genuinely useful for job applications, possible without uploading files, and free for everyone.

  • By email: suggest a feature.
  • On GitHub: open a feature request.

Report a security problem

If you find a way that ApplyReady could expose someone's files or data, please do not open a public issue. Email hello@nbsumit.com with the details. The project’s security policy explains what happens next, including when to expect a reply.

What happens to your feedback

There is no feedback form on this site, no analytics and no error reporting service, so nothing is collected automatically, not even when a tool fails. Emails are read by the maintainer and used only to answer you and improve ApplyReady. Replies usually take 3 to 4 business days. If you would like to go further and help fix things, see how to contribute. For questions rather than reports, see contact and support; the how to use page and the guides answer most common questions.
