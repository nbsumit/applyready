# Content-quality remediation: status

Work in progress on the `content-quality` branch, in response to the AdSense "Low-value content" review.
Goal: fewer, better pages that are clearly original, useful, maintained and trustworthy.
Not merged to main yet.

## Done on this branch

- **Trust pages**
  - New `author/sumit-sengar.html` (Person JSON-LD).
  - New `editorial-policy.html` (sources, measurements, review, corrections, AI use, independence, corrections log).
  - `about.html` rewritten, with the project history taken from git dates.
  - `contact.html` rewritten, with mailto templates and a "do not send documents" note.
  - `privacy.html` has a new Cookies section and Google CMP note, plus a localStorage section.
  - The footer now links About, Author, Editorial policy, Contact, Privacy and Terms on every page.
- **Measurements**
  - Synthetic sample images are in `img/samples/`, generated by `scripts/make-sample-images.py`.
  - The real tool outputs are recorded in `docs/measurements/*.json` (Chromium, 24 Oct 2026).
- **Screenshots**
  - `scripts/guide-screenshots.js` writes annotated WebP screenshots to `img/guides/`.
- **Home page:** an original explainer below the tool, with a worked example using measured numbers.
- **Tool how-tos** below signature-cleaner, passport-photo-print, image-to-pdf and resume:
  - real before/after numbers, common mistakes, and when not to use the tool;
  - FAQs rewritten;
  - unsupported ₹ price claims removed.
- **KB pages**
  - 20 KB and 50 KB keep their own pages, now with official form citations, a measured data table and troubleshooting.
  - 10, 100 and 200 KB are merged into `resize-image-to-kb-limit.html` (anchors `#10kb`, `#100kb`, `#200kb`).
  - The old URLs are noindex stubs with a meta refresh and a canonical to the merged page.
  - Why merge: the three pages differed only by the number. One page with anchored sections avoids thin near-duplicates.
- **Sitemap**
  - `scripts/build-sitemap.js` generates `sitemap.xml` from the indexable pages only.
  - `lastmod` is the date of the last commit that changed the visible text in `<main>`.
- **Tests**
  - `tests/content_tests.js` now understands redirect stubs and noindex pages:
    - stubs must be noindex and canonical to an indexable page;
    - noindex and redirect pages must not be in the sitemap;
    - every sitemap URL must be indexable.
  - The browser and UI tests were updated for the merged KB page.
- **Service worker:** `sw.js` cache bumped to v19 and the precache list updated.
