# Content-quality remediation: report

This branch responds to the AdSense "Low-value content" review. The aim was fewer, better pages: each one original, useful, maintained and trustworthy.
This file records what changed, the prose word count of each page, the claims we could not fully verify, the TODOs for Sumit, and the issues found along the way.

## What changed

- **Trust pages.**
  - New pages: `author/sumit-sengar.html` and `editorial-policy.html`. The editorial policy covers sourcing, measurements, review cadence, corrections, AI use and independence, and has a corrections log.
  - `about.html`, `contact.html` and `privacy.html` were rewritten. Privacy now has a Cookies section and a note on the Google consent message.
  - The footer on every page links About, Author, Editorial policy, Contact, Privacy and Terms.
- **Exam pages** (SSC, UPSC, IBPS, NEET, RRB). Each one now has:
  - a table of official requirements, citing the notice, its date and the section or page for every row;
  - the documented upload steps and portal quirks;
  - a Sources list;
  - a visible "Last checked against" line with a Report an error link.
  - The generic paragraphs repeated across exam pages were removed.
- **Exam presets now follow the notices.**
  - SSC: the page offers the signature only (6.0 × 2.0 cm at 300 DPI), because the photo is captured live.
  - NEET: the signature limit is 10–100 KB.
  - IBPS: thumb impression and declaration presets were added.
  - RRB: custom size only.
  - Where an exam sets no pixel size, the preset's pixel size is labelled "suggested".
- **Hindi pages.**
  - They carry the same verified facts as the English pages.
  - They are `noindex,follow`, have no hreflang, link to each other, and are left out of the sitemap.
- **KB pages.**
  - The 20 KB and 50 KB pages keep their own URLs, now with cited forms, measured data and troubleshooting.
  - The 10, 100 and 200 KB pages are merged into `resize-image-to-kb-limit.html`, with anchored sections. The old URLs are noindex stubs with a meta refresh and a canonical to the merged page.
  - The three pages differed only by the number, so one page with three sections replaces three thin near-duplicates.
- **Tool pages.** The signature cleaner, passport photo print, image to PDF and resume builder each have a how-to below the tool, with measured before/after numbers, common mistakes and when not to use the tool.
- **Home page.** A new explainer below the tool, including a worked example with measured numbers.
- **Guides: merges.** Three overlapping guides were merged into a stronger one, and the old URLs became redirect stubs:
  - why-was-my-photo-rejected → fix-common-photo-upload-errors
  - compression-targets → how-to-reduce-photo-to-50kb-without-blur (now covers any KB limit)
  - image-dimensions-vs-file-size → pixels-cm-inches-dpi-explained
- **Guides: differentiated.**
  - The two PDF guides: combining versus compressing.
  - The summary guide versus the role-by-role guide.
  - The ATS, length, skills and tailoring guides, so each answers one question.
- **Guides: campus placement.** The campus placement overview is `noindex,follow`.
- **Guides: every remaining one** (21 indexable) now has:
  - a byline linking the author page, plus Published and Last updated dates that match its JSON-LD;
  - an original screenshot or SVG diagram;
  - a Sources section;
  - a Report an error link;
  - in-body links to its tool and to related guides.
- **Guides hub.** An introduction, the guides grouped by task, and a last-updated date for each.
- **Linking and sitemap.**
  - Every indexable page is now linked from the body text of at least three other pages.
  - Breadcrumbs, visible and as JSON-LD, are on guides, exam pages, KB pages and the new pages.
  - `sitemap.xml` is generated by `scripts/build-sitemap.js`. It lists indexable canonical pages only, each with the date of its last real content change.
- **Tests.** `tests/content_tests.js` now fails when:
  - a redirect stub or noindex page is in the sitemap, or a sitemap URL is not indexable;
  - a guide lacks its byline, dates, visual, Sources section with an outside link, tool link, guide links or report link;
  - an exam page lacks its Last checked line or sources;
  - an indexable page has fewer than three in-body inbound links.

## Claims that are not fully verified

- **RRB.** No RRB notice (CEN) could be opened: every RRB host failed from the build environment. The RRB page therefore gives no figures and says "Check the latest official notification". Someone on an Indian network should open the current CEN and add the figures with citations.
- **UPSC.** The NDA, CDS and CMS 2026 notices did not load (HTTP 504/403). The UPSC page relies on the portal instructions and the CSE 2026 and ESE 2027 notices, and says so. The portal PDF links contain build hashes and may change when UPSC redeploys the portal.
- **SSC signature size.**
  - Some SSC annexures say 4.0 × 2.0 cm, while the main text of every notice says 6.0 × 2.0 cm. We follow the main text and explain the conflict.
  - 709 × 236 px is our own conversion of 6.0 × 2.0 cm at 300 DPI. It is not a figure SSC publishes.
- **NEET (UG) 2026 conflicts.**
  - Fingerprints: the bulletin says 10–200 KB in one place and 50–300 KB in another.
  - Identity proof: the bulletin says JPG 10–200 KB in one place and 50–300 KB in another.
  - Both conflicts are shown on the page.
- **PAN.** Two Protean pages give different figures. We cite the guideline page linked from the DSC application route. That page does not say which side of the photo is 3.5 cm.
- **Suggested pixel sizes.** These are tool settings, not official sizes, and are labelled as suggestions:
  - UPSC photo: 350 × 450 px
  - NEET photo: 400 × 500 px
  - NEET signature: 300 × 100 px
- **Resume guides.** No Indian government body publishes resume-writing guidance that we could find. The guides cite the UK National Careers Service and say so. The example bullets and summaries are illustrative and labelled as such.
- **Measurements.** All of them were made in Chromium 141 on Linux on 24 October 2026. Other browsers may produce files a few KB larger or smaller.
- **Browser behaviour, untested.** Two statements describe general browser behaviour that we did not measure for every browser:
  - browsers apply a photo's EXIF orientation when drawing it;
  - JPEG files start with FF D8 FF.
- **General advice without a citation:**
  - portals can be slow near the deadline;
  - chat apps recompress photos.
- **Campus placement overview.** Kept, but `noindex`. It has not been checked against official sources.

## Issues found (not fixed here)

- **Compress PDF can make a file larger.**
  - Example: a 136.3 KB PDF compressed with a 200 KB target came out at 161.5 KB, labelled "0% smaller". The cause is that `js/compress-pdf.js` re-encodes every page, even when the input already fits.
  - The compress guide now advises compressing only files that are over the limit.
  - Suggested fix: return the original file when it is already at or under the target, and never offer an output larger than the input.
- **NEET signature figure.** The 2026 bulletin states 10–100 KB, which differs from the earlier instruction to show "typically 10–50 KB". The page follows the bulletin, as the hard rule on official sources requires.
- **UPSC and SSC removals.**
  - UPSC: the pixel range, the 10-day rule and the name-and-date rule from the 3b96f62 baseline were removed, because the current UPSC instructions do not contain them.
  - SSC: the photo preset was removed, because SSC captures the photo live.
