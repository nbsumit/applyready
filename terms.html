2. What ApplyReady is

ApplyReady is a free collection of tools that run in your web browser to help you prepare job and other applications: a resume builder, image and signature resizers and converters, PDF tools, document tools, batch processing and temporary applicant workspaces. Every feature is free for everyone. There are no accounts, paid plans, usage quotas or daily caps, and contributing money does not unlock anything. Each tool states its own technical limits, such as the largest file it accepts, which keep the browser from running out of memory.

Your files are processed on your own device and are not uploaded to us (see the privacy policy). You keep all rights to the text, images and documents you use with ApplyReady, and you are responsible for having the right to use them. Saved resumes are kept in your browser's storage on your device; we cannot see, recover or restore them, so keep your own backups.

Always check the current requirements of the place you are applying to before you submit.
