How it works

Choose the position, the format and the first number. The preview shows the first numbered page exactly as it will come out, because it is made by the same code as the download. With No number on the first page on, the cover is left blank and numbering starts on page 2 with your first number, and "of 10" counts only the numbered pages. Numbers are placed by how each page is seen, so they stay upright at the bottom of a page that is stored turned sideways.

Numbers are drawn on top of each page in the standard Helvetica font, which every PDF reader has. Everything else on the page is left as it was. Put the pages in order first with Organise PDF pages or Merge PDF; to stamp words such as "Self-attested" on every page, use Watermark PDF.

Limits

Files up to 100 MB. The standard PDF font writes digits and the words used here; it cannot write every script. A number placed where the page already has text will overlap it, so pick a position with empty space, which the preview helps you check.

Your files are processed in this browser and never leave your device. Closing the tab clears them.

Questions

Can I leave the cover page without a number?

Yes. Turn on No number on the first page. Page 2 then shows your first number, usually 1, and "Page 1 of N" counts only the numbered pages.

Can numbering start at a number other than 1?

Yes. Type it in First number. This is useful when the PDF continues another document, for example starting at 5.

Will the numbers cover my text?

They are drawn on top of the page, so they can overlap text that is already there. Check the preview and choose a position or a larger distance from the edge where the page is empty.

What happens with landscape or turned pages?

Each page is numbered as it is seen, so the number sits at the bottom of a landscape page and reads upright even when the page is stored turned.

Which formats are available?

1, 2, 3; Page 1; Page 1 of 10; and 1 / 10, in sizes from 8 to 16 points and in black, grey, blue or red.

Is my PDF uploaded?

No. The numbers are added by your browser on this device.
