Common Reasons SSC Rejects Applications

  • Oversized Photos: Photos exceeding 50 KB or signatures exceeding 20 KB are instantly blocked by the online portal validator.
  • Blurry Signatures: Downsampling a low-contrast photo without proper cropping leads to illegible ink strokes. Use our unconstrained cropper to frame only the ink area.
  • Caps & Spectacles: Selfies or candid photos wearing caps, masks, or tinted glasses are flagged during scrutiny.
  • Block Letters Signature: Signing in ALL-CAPS (e.g. "SUMIT") violates SSC paragraph 15 guidelines. Sign in your natural cursive or running hand.
  • Outdated Photographs: SSC guidelines state that the photograph must not be more than three months old from the notification publication date.

What the SSC limits mean for your files

The sizes above are easy to read and hard to judge. Dividing each file-size range by the number of pixels shows how much detail each pixel can carry, and where the real risk is: a file that is too large, or one that comes out too small.

FileDimensionsSize rangeBits per pixel (low to high)What to watch
Passport photo350 x 45020 to 50 KB1.0 to 2.6Aim for the upper half of the range. Near 20 KB the face looks soft.
Signature400 x 20010 to 20 KB1.0 to 2.0Plenty of room at the top end. Check the file is not below 10 KB: the preset pads it if needed.

Two files, two different risks. The photo is limited by the top of its range: a 350 x 450 portrait at 20 KB gets about one bit per pixel, while at 50 KB it gets about 2.6. So work towards the 40 to 50 KB end. The signature has the opposite risk: a clean black-on-white signature has little detail to store and can come out smaller than 10 KB.

What the preset does for you. The photo and signature presets above carry both the minimum and the maximum, and the tool searches for the highest quality that fits. If a very clean signature comes out below the minimum, the tool adds harmless metadata to the file so the picture is unchanged and the size is in range.

Always confirm the figures against the current official notification linked above before you upload. For the method behind these numbers, see pixels, centimetres and DPI and common upload errors and fixes.

Frequently Asked Questions: SSC Image Formatting

Quick answers to clear portal upload rejections.

What is the exact photo size for SSC CGL, CHSL, and MTS?

The photograph must be in JPEG format with a file size strictly between 20 KB and 50 KB. The visual dimensions should be about 3.5 cm width by 4.5 cm height (around 350 × 450 pixels) with a plain white or light background.

What are the signature requirements for SSC exams?

The scanned signature must be between 10 KB and 20 KB in JPEG format, with dimensions of 4.0 cm width by 2.0 cm height (about 400 × 200 pixels). Sign with a black or dark blue pen on white unruled paper in running handwriting.
