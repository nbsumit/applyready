SSC Signature Size and Live Photo Rules, 2026

SSC no longer takes an uploaded photo: its application module captures one live. The only image you prepare is the signature. Below is what the 2025 and 2026 notices say, with the section and page for each rule.

What the SSC notices ask for

SSC takes applications on ssc.gov.in and in its mySSC app, and the photo and signature rules are written almost the same way in every notice we read: CGL, CHSL, MTS, GD, CPO, JE, Stenographer and Selection Post. The figures below are quoted from those notices; each row names the section and page.

ItemWhat the notice saysSource
PhotographNot a file. "The application module has been designed to capture a photograph of the candidate filling up the application form," through a webcam, a mobile device or the mySSC app. No KB limit, size or format is given, because nothing is uploaded.CGL 2026 notice (F. 6.0 cm or 4.0 cm? The step-by-step annexures of some notices, including CGL 2026 (p. 71), CHSL 2026 (p. 53) and CPO 2026 (p. 32), give the signature as "about 4.0 cm (Width) x 2.0 cm (Height)". The main text of every notice, and the annexures of the GD 2026, JE 2026 and MTS 2025 notices, say 6.0 × 2.0 cm. This page and its preset follow the main text. Both are approximate ("about"), and the 10 to 20 KB range and JPEG format are the same in every version.

Where 709 × 236 px comes from. SSC does not state a pixel size. The preset converts the GD 2026 figure: 6.0 cm ÷ 2.54 × 300 DPI = 709 px wide, and 2.0 cm at 300 DPI = 236 px high. If your notice gives a different size, choose Custom size and enter it, in centimetres if you prefer.

The live photo: what SSC checks

Because the photo is taken inside the application, the preparation is about the room, not the file. Each notice lists the same five instructions (CGL 2026, para 9.4, p. 18):

  1. "Find a place with good light and plain background."
  2. "Ensure the camera is at eye level before taking the photo."
  3. "Position himself directly in front of the camera and look straight ahead."
  4. "Ensure that his face is fully inside the prescribed area delineated by the camera and that it is neither too close nor too far."
  5. "Candidates should not wear a cap, mask or glasses/spectacles while capturing the photo."

The ban covers all spectacles, not only dark glasses. The MTS 2025 notice (para 10.4, p. 13) adds earphones and headphones, and the GD 2026 notice (Annexure-II, item 20(g)) says "Do not wear earphones or any device while capturing Photos."

Two rules decide whether an application survives scrutiny. First, photographing an existing photo is not allowed: "All such applications where the photographs of his/her pre-existing photograph is captured will be rejected" (CGL 2026, para 9.5). Second, "the appearance of a candidate in the examination should be as per the photograph in the application form" (para 9.5), so take the photo looking the way you will look on exam day.

SSC's advisory on photos and signatures (15 August 2024, p. 3) lists the photo faults it rejects: "Photo without plain background", "Candidates wearing caps", "Candidates taking photos without shirts", "Photo not sufficiently bright" and "Photo is blurred".

How the upload step works on ssc.gov.in

  1. One-Time Registration on the new site. An OTR made on the old ssc.nic.in "will not be functional for the new website"; register again on ssc.gov.in (CGL 2026, para 9.2).
  2. Aadhaar authentication, if you choose it. OTR has been Aadhaar-enabled since 2 June 2025 (SSC notice of 9 May 2025). Applications of candidates who opt for it "will not be rejected on the ground that photograph and/or signature uploaded by the candidate are not as per prescribed standards" (CGL 2026, para 9.3).
  3. The upload page has three steps in order: face authentication status, "Capture Your Photograph" with a Capture Live Photo button, then "Upload Your Signature" (screenshot in the GD 2026 notice, Annexure-II, p. 56).
  4. No working webcam? The Upload Documents page shows a QR code for installing the mySSC app to take the photo on a phone (CGL 2026, Annexure-IV, p. 71).
  5. Or apply entirely in the app. Since June 2025 the whole application can be completed in mySSC, which "works only on Android phones with version 11 or higher" and needs the Aadhaar Face RD app for face authentication (SSC notice of 2 June 2025).
  6. Check before you submit. Candidates "should also check that photograph and signature are meeting all the above requirements" (CGL 2026, para 9.11).

This change dates from April 2024, when SSC moved from an uploaded photo to a live one: "This is a change from the earlier dispensation wherein the candidates were required to upload a photograph already captured" (notice of 1 April 2024). A photo size in KB from material written before then describes the old system.

Preparing the signature file

SSC's advisory names the most common signature fault: "The major reasons for rejection of signatures are 'miniature' signatures. Candidates are advised to cut the box and then sign within the box such that signature occupies at least 80% of the box." A practical way to do this is to draw a 6 × 2 cm box on white paper, sign inside it so the ink nearly touches the sides, photograph or scan it, and crop to the box.

We ran this page's preset on the synthetic signature photo in our sample set (1600 × 900 px). At 709 × 236 px it was 33.1 KB at quality 0.95, so the tool lowered the quality to 0.89 and produced an 18.6 KB file (19,052 bytes), inside the range. The same signature run through the signature cleaner first fitted at quality 0.95 with 17.5 KB, because a white background compresses better than grey paper. Results in your browser can differ by a few kilobytes.

If a portal message says the file is under 10 KB, enter 10 in Minimum (KB) under Size, file limit and format; the tool pads the JPEG with comment data to reach it without changing the picture. For more on limits around this size, see resizing to 20 KB and common upload errors.

Frequently asked questions: SSC

Do I upload a photo for SSC CGL or CHSL 2026?

No. The 2026 notices say the application module captures a live photograph through a webcam, a mobile device or the mySSC app (CGL 2026, para 9.4). You do not upload a photo file, so no photo KB limit applies.

What size should the SSC signature be?

A JPEG/JPG file of 10 to 20 KB, about 6.0 cm wide and 2.0 cm high (CGL 2026, para 9.6). The GD 2026 notice adds "at a resolution of 300 DPI". SSC gives no pixel size; 6.0 × 2.0 cm at 300 DPI works out to 709 × 236 px, which is what this page's preset uses.

Why do some SSC documents say 4.0 × 2.0 cm?

The application-instruction annexures of some notices, such as CGL 2026 (p. 71) and CHSL 2026 (p. 53), say about 4.0 × 2.0 cm, while the main text of every notice says about 6.0 × 2.0 cm. Both are approximate and the 10 to 20 KB JPEG rule is the same. Follow the figure in the notice for your exam if it differs.

Can I wear spectacles in the SSC live photo?

No. The notices say candidates "should not wear a cap, mask or glasses/spectacles while capturing the photo". The MTS 2025 and GD 2026 notices also rule out earphones and headphones.

My webcam does not work. How do I take the SSC photo?

The Upload Documents page has a QR code for installing the mySSC app, which can capture the photo on an Android phone (CGL 2026, Annexure-IV). Since June 2025 the whole application can also be filled in the app.

Is my signature uploaded to ApplyReady?

No. The resizing runs in your browser, on your device. Only the SSC portal receives the file, when you upload it there.
